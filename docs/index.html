Equivariant elliptic cohomology via loop spaces

Emile Bouaziz, Adeel A. Khan · 2611.05707
title: equivariantabstract: ring spectrabstract: e_infinity-ringmath.AGmath.ATmath.RT
Abstract

We construct a cohomology theory $H_A(-)$ canonically attached to any 1-dimensional commutative group scheme A over a connective $E_\infty$-ring. The additive group in characteristic zero gives 2-periodic de Rham cohomology, the multiplicative group gives topological periodic cyclic homology, and an elliptic curve gives elliptic cohomology. The construction extends to the G-equivariant setting for an algebraic group G, yielding in the elliptic case a theory of equivariant elliptic cohomology on algebraic varieties with G-action that takes values in sheaves on the stack of G-bundles. Over the complex numbers, and for G connected reductive, this lifts Grojnowski's theory from the moduli space of G-bundles to the stack. The key player is the category of perfect complexes on A with 0-dimensional support, equipped with the convolution product induced by the group law of A. It plays the role of a categorified circle and is used to define a loop space $L_A^G(X)$, whose structure sheaf gives rise to $H_A^G(X)$ after periodization. Sheaves on $L_A^G(X)$ equivariant under loop rotation provide a category of coefficients for $H_A^G(-)$ in the same way that D-modules provide coefficients for de Rham cohomology. At the point X = *, the theory also gives rise to an $E_\infty$-ring spectrum (or sheaf thereof) $K_A$, which recovers complex K-theory for $A = \mathbf{G}_m$, and the sheaf $\mathcal{O}^{top}$ of topological modular forms for the universal elliptic curve.

Koszul duality and homotopy algebras for operads over quivers

Hang Yuan · 2611.06165
title: koszul dualabstract: infinity categorabstract: higher operadmath.ATmath.CTmath.QA
Abstract

For a quiver $Q$, we introduce and study $Q$-operads (or operads over $Q$), defined as a special sort of $\mathbf{fc}$-multicategories or virtual double categories, in which the directions of the arrows of $Q$ encode the permitted input orderings. In this higher operadic framework, we construct the bar--cobar adjunction and develop a theory of Koszul duality and homotopy algebras, together with compatibility results under changes of quiver. We treat dg categories and their left modules, right modules, and bimodules as algebras over quiver-operads. We prove that these operads are Koszul and that their resolutions govern the corresponding $A_\infty$ categories and modules. We also categorify Sagave's notion of derived $A_\infty$ algebras and introduce modules over derived $A_\infty$ algebras and categories. Finally, we explain that restriction along a folding of quivers constructs diagonal $A_\infty$ bimodules, while extension along an inclusion of quivers realizes two-sided bar models of derived tensor products.

Perfect Forms and Torsion Bounds in Algebraic $K$-Theory over Totally Real and CM Fields

Alejandro de la Torre Dur\'an · 2611.06300
title: algebraic k-theormath.KTmath.GT
Abstract

We extend the method of Van Woerden \cite{woerden} to totally real and CM number fields to obtain an upper bound for the number of $\GLo-$equivalence classes of perfect forms. Moreover, by considering appropriate barycentric subdivisions of the Voronoi tessellation, we extend these estimates to the number of $\GLo$-orbits of $k$-dimensional faces of the Voronoi tessellation. As a consequence, we improve the asymptotic order of the bounds for the torsion part of $K_m(\rint)$ obtained by Soul\'e \cite{soule_hermitian_bounds} and Houriet \cite{Juliet_PHD}. We emphasise the improvement of the general bound for $F=\mathbb Q$, unchanged since Soul\'e's work, and the independence of the discriminant exponent from the field degree for totally real and CM fields.

Floer theory on based loops and fundamental groups of Lagrangians in $\mathbb{C}^n$

Shuhao Li · 2611.06574
math.SGmath.ATmath.GTcross
Abstract

We obtain new constraints on the topology of Lagrangian submanifolds in the standard symplectic vector space $\mathbb{C}^n$ using Floer theory on the based loop space, generalizing a theorem of Fukaya-Irie. In particular, we show that non-trivial connected sums of closed, spin, aspherical manifolds of even dimensions do not admit Lagrangian embeddings into $\mathbb{C}^n$. A feature of the proof is that the obstructedness of the Floer theory is used to constrain the fundamental group of the Lagrangian.

Homology and Topology of Partially Ordered Fields

Hanzhen Bao · 2611.06261
math.RAmath.ACmath.ATcross
Abstract

We develop a unified homology theory for directed partial orders (DPOs) and lattice-ordered positive cones on fields of characteristic zero. With the underlying field and its operations fixed, we define order?carrying spaces independently by topological conditions, establish a bijection with DPO positive cones, and characterize lattice orders by intersections of minimal open neighborhoods. A unified comparison theorem identifies graded Ext for region ideals with sheaf cohomology and multiplicative Tor with the reduced singular homology of truncated regions, giving a topological interpretation of the existence and preservation of reduced decompositions. We obtain canonical cohomological obstructions to lattice ordering, prove that a coefficient module for positive multiplication is flat if and only if the multiplier has a positive inverse, and show that every strict extension of positive cones on the same field produces a nonzero first Tor obstruction. Finally, we place the concrete lattice recently constructed by Bao-Zhang within this framework.

Magnitude, path and coarse homology

Yasuhiko Asao · 2611.05972
math.AT
Abstract

In this article, we introduce Borel--Moore and uniformly finite versions of magnitude homology and path homology for locally finite graphs by allowing possibly infinite chains subject to local finiteness and boundedness conditions. In both settings, we construct magnitude--path spectral sequences extending the spectral sequence studied by the author. Their E^1-pages are given by the corresponding magnitude homologies, their E^2-pages contain the corresponding path homologies on the p-axis, and they converge to coarse homology and uniformly finite homology, respectively, in the sense of Nowak--Yu. Moreover, the E^{r+1}-page is invariant under r- homotopy, whereas the homology to which the spectral sequence converges is invariant under coarse equivalence. Thus the spectral sequences interpolate between small-scale and large-scale geometry. We also show that the Borel--Moore and uniformly finite magnitude homologies are products and restricted products, respectively, of ordinary magnitude homology groups with a fixed initial point. Hence the E^1-page is assembled from pointwise magnitude-homological data, providing a mechanism for combining local information into global large-scale invariants. As applications, we establish Mayer--Vietoris sequences for magnitude homology, path homology, the associated spectral-sequence pages, and coarse and uniformly finite homology. We also show that diagonality of ordinary magnitude homology implies degeneration at the E^2-page, yielding isomorphisms between path homology and the corresponding coarse homology. Finally, we discuss applications to Cayley graphs of graph products, including right-angled Artin groups and right-angled Coxeter groups.

Projected and Reflected Sliced Wasserstein Kernels for Persistence Diagrams

Juli\'an Uribe Casta\~neda, Mircea Petrache · 2611.06619
cs.CGmath.ATcross
Abstract

Persistence diagrams do not naturally live in a Hilbert space. Carri\`ere, Cuturi, and Oudot embedded them in kernel methods through the Sliced Wasserstein kernel, built from the Sliced Wasserstein distance $\mathrm{SW}_1$, which is conditionally negative definite (CND). We study the projected ($\mathrm{SW}_p$) and reflected ($\mathrm{RW}_p$) Sliced Wasserstein distances for every order $p\ge1$, and compare them with the $p$-Wasserstein distance $d_p$ and with a reflected variant $d_p^r$, in which projection onto the diagonal is replaced by reflection across it. We show that $2^{1/p}d_p\le d_p^r\le 2d_p$ with sharp constants, so that $d_1^r=2d_1$ and $d_p^r$ inherits the stability of $d_p$, although it is a metric only for $p=1$. The distances $\mathrm{SW}_1$ and $\mathrm{RW}_1$ are CND metrics, and $\mathrm{RW}_1$ gives rise to a new positive definite kernel, stable with respect to $d_1$. For $p>1$, neither $\mathrm{SW}_p$ nor $\mathrm{RW}_p$ satisfies the triangle inequality, and rigorous interval-arithmetic certificates prove that $\mathrm{SW}_2$ and $\mathrm{RW}_2$ are not CND; numerical experiments suggest that the CND property fails for every $p$ above some $p^*\in(1,1.2]$. On diagrams with at most $N$ points, $\mathrm{SW}_p$ and $\mathrm{RW}_p$ are metrically equivalent to $d_p$ and $d_p^r$, with sharp upper constants. On an orbit recognition benchmark, the kernels built from $\mathrm{SW}_1$ and $\mathrm{RW}_1$ reach about 85% accuracy, outperform the persistence scale space kernel, perform on par with the persistence weighted Gaussian kernel, and are about one percentage point below the kernel built from $\mathrm{SW}_2$, which is not positive definite.
