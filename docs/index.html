On the Anderson Dual of $H\underline{\mathbb Z}$ and $H\underline{\mathbb F}_2$

Hana Jia Kong, Achim Krause, Yutao Liu, XiaoLin Danny Shi, Zhouli Xu · 2610.35796
author: Achim Krauseauthor: Zhouli Xutitle: anderson dualmath.AT
Abstract

In this paper, we give complete classifications of the finite groups $G$ for which the constant Eilenberg--Mac Lane spectra $H\underline{\mathbb Z}$ and $H\underline{\mathbb F}_2$ are Anderson self-dual up to a virtual representation shift, and we determine all such shifts. For both of these spectra, we determine the representation periodicity lattice for every finite group. As applications, we obtain $RO(G)$-graded universal coefficient theorems relating equivariant homology and cohomology, together with formulas for the Anderson duals of $H\underline{\mathbb Z}$-modules. In the $H\underline{\mathbb F}_2$ self-dual cases, we identify the graded $\mathbb F_2$-linear dual of the coefficient ring $\pi_\star^G H\underline{\mathbb F}_2$ with a representation shift of the ring as a module over itself. This gives perfect multiplication pairings and graded self-injectivity.

author: Sanath K. Devalapurkartitle: e_n-algebraabstract: k(1)-localmath.ATmath.AG
Abstract

We study $\mathbf{E}_n$-algebras with ramification in their $\pi_0$ and in particular show that the existence of ramification places strong limitations on their chromatic behavior. Elmendorf's Theorem for $\infty$-categories

Peter Rowley · 2610.33183
title: infinity-categormath.ATmath.RT
Abstract

Motivated by Teleman's study of gauged topological quantum field theories, our main result is an analog of Elmendorf's theorem in the setting of simplicial group actions on $\infty$-categories. In particular, we prove that if $G$ is a simplicial group, there is a Quillen equivalence between the homotopy category of $\infty$-categories with $G$-action and the homotopy category of $\infty$-category-valued presheaves on the orbit category of $G$. We also show an analog of Whitehead's theorem in this setting.

Homological and homotopical epimorphisms in monoidal $\infty$-categories

Federico Bambozzi · 2610.34592
title: infinity-categormath.CTmath.AGmath.RA
Abstract

We compare homological and homotopical epimorphisms of associative algebras in monoidal $\infty$-categories. Assume that geometric realizations and pushouts of associative algebras exist and that tensoring preserves geometric realizations. Then every homological epimorphism is homotopical. If, in addition, the ambient category is additive and tensoring preserves finite coproducts, the converse holds. This extends the result of Chuang and Lazarev for dg-algebras. In the symmetric monoidal setting, we discuss consequences for centers and commutative refinements, as well as applications to derived analytic geometry.

On Reed-Muller-Type Codes Associated to Proto-Exact Categories

Jeremy Edison, Alexander Sistko · 2610.33903
title: exact categormath.CTmath.COmath.RT
Abstract

In this paper, we show how to associate a family of binary linear block codes to the class of admissibly cofinitary objects in any proto-exact category with rank function. This family naturally generalizes the well-studied family of binary Reed-Muller codes, and recovers this classical family for suitable choices of category. We also expand the list of known examples of proto-abelian categories to include incidence categories of posets. As an application of this result, we construct a binormal, combinatorial, proto-abelian category of Kashiwara crystals associated to any root system. In the case that the proto-exact category is a category of $\mathbb{F}_1$-representations of quivers, we show how to reconstruct our family via the language of algebraic geometry codes, in particular motivating an extension to arbitrary finite fields. We provide explicit computations of the dimension and minimum distance for select families of $\mathbb{F}_1$-representations of quivers.

Semiadditive Power Operations in Chromatic Height $1$

Beckham Myers · 2610.34992
title: power operationabstract: k(1)-localabstract: algebraic k-theormath.AT
Abstract

We recast the theory of power operations in chromatic height $1$ using higher semiadditivity. In particular, we show that if the height $1$ cyclotomic extension of a commutative algebra $R$ is Galois, then power operations on $R$-algebras are exactly $\delta$-ring operations. We also show that semiadditive power operations on chromatically localized algebraic $K$-theory agree with power operations defined using polynomial functoriality. As an application, we deduce that the map from the $K$-theory of a $\mathbb{Q}$-linear symmetric monoidal category to its $K(1)$-localization is a $\delta$-ring homomorphism on the level of underlying spaces. It is well known that operations on the $K$-theory of $\mathbb{Q}$-linear symmetric monoidal categories are $\lambda$-ring operations, and since $\delta$-rings are `$p$-typical' $\lambda$-rings, this is an instance of the redshift philosophy.

Spectra, Growth, and Localization in Half-Bounded Tensor Triangular Geometry

Liangyu Zhu · 2610.34469
title: tensor triangularabstract: balmer spectrmath.AGmath.ACmath.ATmath.CT
Abstract

Over a noetherian scheme, classical cohomological support determines thick tensor ideals of perfect complexes. In half-bounded derived categories, complexes with the same support can generate different radical thick tensor ideals. We study how these distinctions affect the Balmer spectrum and localization. For a commutative noetherian ring $R$, we prove that $\operatorname{Spc}(\mathbf D^-_{\mathrm{fg}}(R))$ is noetherian if and only if $R$ is artinian. For a nonempty noetherian scheme $X$, the corresponding condition is $\dim X=0$. For such a ring $R$ and a finite group $G$, $\operatorname{Spc}(\mathbf D^-_{\mathrm{fg}}(RG))$ is noetherian if and only if $R$ is artinian and $|G|$ is invertible in $R$. Using the Sanders--Zhang classification over a discrete valuation ring, we compare objects whose annihilator exponents grow at different rates in different degrees. We lift the relevant generation relations before changing coefficients and obtain spectral retractions. These comparisons persist after thick quotients and algebraic localization under explicit uniform annihilation and nonvanishing conditions. A second argument uses nonnilpotent classes of positive degree and passes from cyclic subgroups to finite groups. Applications include relative results for schemes and bounded-below spectra with degreewise finitely generated homotopy. We also describe the cohomological information shared by the resulting ideals. The localization comparisons for bounded-below complexes of finitely generated modules and for bounded-below derived objects with finitely generated cohomology are established separately.

Stable homotopy theory of higher categories with adjoints

Hadrian Heine · 2610.35861
title: higher categortitle: stable homotopymath.AT
Abstract

We demonstrate that higher categories with adjoints behave like homotopy types. By the homotopy hypothesis, homotopy types can be regarded as higher categories in which all cells are invertible. We show that replacing invertibility by the existence of higher adjoints still supports a homotopy theory that is at least as rich as classical homotopy theory. The basic invariants of this theory are the homotopy monoidal categories \[ \Pi_n(X,x)=\operatorname{Ho}\bigl(\Omega^n(X,x)\bigr) \] associated to a based $(\infty,\infty)$-category with adjoints $(X,x)$. These invariants are monoidal categories with duals and replace iterated loops with iterated endomorphisms. The classical homotopy groups of the classifying space are recovered as the isomorphism classes of objects in the groupoid completion. We prove a Whitehead theorem for homotopy monoidal categories and show that fibrations of higher categories give rise to long oriented exact sequences of homotopy monoidal categories. To develop homological algebra of higher categories with adjoints, we prove an equivalence between skeletal sequences of categorical spectra with adjoints and categorical chain complexes, extending a result outlined by Lurie in the proof of the cobordism hypothesis.

There is a long tradition of constructing smooth projective varieties in positive characteristic with non-degenerate Hodge-to-de Rham spectral sequences. Voevodsky's motives and zeroth slice of motivic sphere

Bo Zhang · 2610.34898
title: motivic spheretitle: motivicmath.AGmath.KT
Abstract

Let $S$ be a noetherian scheme of finite Krull dimension and let $R$ be a commutative ring in which every positive residue characteristic of $S$ is invertible. We prove that Voevodsky's stable Nisnevich category of motives with $R$-coefficients is canonically equivalent to modules over the zeroth slice of the motivic sphere with derived $R$-coefficients. The comparison identifies the corresponding commutative coefficient algebras and is compatible with arbitrary pullback. The theorem applies to singular and nonreduced schemes, including mixed-characteristic bases, and requires no excellence or finite-type hypothesis. Its special cases include rational coefficients over arbitrary bases, arbitrary coefficients over $\mathbb Q$-schemes, and $\mathbb Z[1/p]$-algebra coefficients over $\mathbb Z_{(p)}$-schemes. The proof establishes base change for finite-cycle spectra using permutation quotients, families of cycles, and arithmetic power homotopies, then passes to general bases by universal homeomorphism invariance and continuity.

Construction of holomorphic SCFTs with minimal index

Du Pei · 2610.33165
hep-thmath-phmath.ATmath.MPcross
Abstract

It has been an open question whether there exist holomorphic VOAs with N=1 supersymmetry and Witten index 1 for any positive central charge c. The existence of such a theory with c=288 is closely related to the 576-periodicity of the TMF spectrum via the Segal-Stolz-Teichner conjecture. Here we report the construction of thousands of such theories by extending the approach initiated by Gaiotto and Johnson-Freyd via ternary codes. In addition, for every positive integer k, we also construct holomorphic N=1 VOAs of central charge c=12k with index 24/gcd(24,k) -- conjectured to be the minimum -- in a computer-aided search carried out by AI coding agents. We overcome the exponential-time barrier by using chains of smaller cells to build ternary codes, whose indices can then be evaluated via a transfer-matrix technique, and we use a symmetric orbifold construction to reduce the problem of k=12 and 24 to that of k=6. Along the way, we also establish several results on ternary codes and their indices. Using an observation relating codes and Landau-Ginzburg orbifold models, we argue that the non-chiral doubles of the ternary code VOAs can always be gapped supersymmetrically.

The flipping principle for boundedly acyclic covers and colorings

Pietro Capovilla, Alberto Casali, Marco Moraschini · 2610.35222
math.ATmath.GRmath.GT
Abstract

Based on the theory of multicomplexes and other ideas by Gromov, we describe the flipping principle. This is an easy geometric criterion that forces the vanishing of the Gromov norm of homology classes. We use it to provide proofs based on a common geometric principle of the Vanishing Theorem and the Amenable Reduction Lemma by Gromov in the case of amenable and boundedly acyclic covers and colorings. We also strengthen the conclusion of the Amenable Reduction Lemma by showing that it implies the $\ell^1$-invisibility of the homology classes and not only the vanishing of their Gromov norm.
