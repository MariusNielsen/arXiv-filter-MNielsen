An equivariant index theorem for the Heisenberg calculus

Alexander Gorokhovsky, Erik van Erp · 2611.31538
title: equivariantmath.FAmath.KTmath.OAcross
Abstract

We derive a local fixed-point formula for the equivariant index of Heisenberg elliptic operators on closed contact manifolds equipped with an action of a compact Lie group preserving the contact form. The formula expresses the character of the equivariant index in terms of a cyclic-cohomological character of the noncommutative Heisenberg principal symbol. The main new ingredient is the construction of traces, one on each fixed-point set, defined by integration over twisted conjugacy classes in the Heisenberg group. The proof proceeds by a K-theoretic reduction to the equivariant index theorem of our paper "Toeplitz operators on contact manifolds and equivariant K-homology".

An internal approach to symmetric monoidal $(\infty,n)$-categories

Arghan Dutta · 2611.33107
title: (infinity,n)abstract: cocartesian fibrationmath.CTmath.AT
Abstract

We propose a definition of symmetric monoidal $(\infty,n)$-categories as special left double $(\infty,n)$-fibrations over the category of finite pointed sets. The definition makes sense in a general model of $(\infty,n)$-categories, under a mild condition on its discrete objects, and when the model is left proper and combinatorial we construct model structures whose fibrant objects are exactly these fibrations. For $n=1$ we show that the definition recovers Lurie's symmetric monoidal $(\infty,1)$-categories: the homotopy theory of special left double $(\infty,1)$-fibrations is equivalent to that of special coCartesian fibrations over the category of finite pointed sets. The equivalence is realized by an explicit Quillen equivalence, and two constructions, the staircase and coCartesian strings, translate between the two descriptions.

The Borel Topos

Joseph Helfer, Patrick Lutz, Forte Shinko · 2611.31809
title: toposmath.LOmath.CTcross
Abstract

We construct a topos for Borel mathematics: statements which are true in Borel mathemaics correspond in a precise way to statements which are true in the internal logic of this topos. We investigate several other properties of this topos, including proving that it satisfies a Borel analogue of the Church-Turing thesis: the statement that every function on a standard Borel space is Borel. We also show that an earlier construction of a similar "Borel topos" by van Osdol does not suit these purposes because of an error in that paper.

Abstract

The positive-characteristic analogue of motivic cohomology with coefficients in an Anderson A-motive M over a function field of transcendence degree one was introduced in an earlier work of the author. Hochschild operations and other invariants of stable equivalences of Morita type

Shengyong Pan · 2611.32253
abstract: algebraic k-theormath.RTmath.CT
Abstract

We study stable equivalences defined by bimodule tensor identities over a commutative ring $R$. For the $R$-split exact structure, these identities preserve positive Hochschild homology and positive Hochschild cohomology with its cup product, Gerstenhaber bracket and, in prime characteristic, restricted power. A triangular comparison also identifies the relative singular Hochschild cochain complexes as $B_\infty$-algebras. For reduced pairs of finite-dimensional Frobenius algebras over an algebraically closed field, with diagonalizable Nakayama automorphisms, we construct compatible Frobenius functionals and compare the Tate--Hochschild BV operators in all degrees. For flat algebras over $R$ we give a Hochschild-cohomological criterion for comparing marked associative deformations. It applies to biadjoint Morita-type pairs and, by reduction, to all stable equivalences of Morita type between finite-dimensional algebras over any field. We compare the Bezrukavnikov--Ginzburg maps in characteristic zero by using a common triangular deformation or by lifting a two-sided tilting complex. We also compare marked module deformations over local Artin rings and their augmented universal rings. Further applications concern connected outer automorphism group schemes over perfect fields, the kernels of the quotient maps on ordinary algebraic $K$-theory, and the nonconnective $K$-theory of enhanced relative singularity categories. We construct biadjoint representatives over Henselian Noetherian local rings and relative stable tensor functors for almost $\nu$-stable derived equivalences. We also answer a question of Abe and Hoshino about preservation of the Gorenstein condition in a fixed codimension.

abstract: presentablemath.OAmath.CTmath.FAcross
Abstract

In a recent paper, Tobias Fritz and the second author identified $\sigma C^*$-algebras as a tentative noncommutative analogue of measurable spaces. We advance this analogy by establishing monadicity of $\sigma C^*$-algebras over $C^*$-algebras and proving several results of interest to both the category theory and operator algebra communities. In detail, we show that: - The associated probabilistic morphisms, $\sigma$-cpu maps, admit a description via a comonad closely related to the Giry monad. Cup products with volume classes in bounded cohomology

Michelle Bucher, Roberto Frigerio · 2611.32555
math.GTmath.ATmath.DGcross
Abstract

Let $M$ be a complete Riemannian $n$-manifold, $n\geq 2$, and suppose that either $M$ is negatively curved, or that it is a locally symmetric space of non-compact type. Via integration over straight simplices, every top-degree bounded differential form $\omega\in \Omega_b^n(M)$ defines a bounded cohomology class in $H^n_b(M)$. We prove that every such class belongs to the annihilator of the cup product in the whole graded algebra $H^{\geq 1}_b(M)$. In the case of closed orientable surfaces, this generalizes a result of Marasco, and, via transfer, provides a new proof of the fact that the cup product of the bounded Euler class of $SL(2,\mathbb{R})$ with itself vanishes. Our result also applies to degree-3 bounded classes constructed by Soma and Farre, thus showing that, for surface groups and for free groups, the annihilator of the cup product is infinite dimensional also in degree 3.

Exact, total and filtered small categories

Floris Alderliefste · 2611.01026
math.CTmath.ATmath.COHexagonAI-generated (no human author claimed)
Abstract

For a non-empty connected small category we compare four properties: being filtered; exactness of colimits of abelian groups; Grothendieck's total asphericity, which says that colimits of simplicial sets over the category are homotopy colimits; and being quasi-filtered, which means that two arrows with a common source can be completed to a commutative square and that two parallel arrows are equalized by an integral combination of arrows whose coefficients sum to 1. Each implies the next. We give a category of finite ordered hypergraphs that is totally aspherical and whose colimits are exact over no non-zero ring, and we show that the category of finite ordered two-graphs (sets of triples that meet every four-element set in an even number of triples) is quasi-filtered and not totally aspherical. With the example of Isbell and Mitchell, the three implications are strict. A conjecture in Grothendieck's Les Dérivateurs, that totally aspherical categories are filtered, is therefore false; it holds for sifted categories, those over which colimits of sets commute with finite products.

We construct a Poisson bracket on the cohomology of locally constant prefactorization algebras on $\mathbb{R}^m$ that provides an obstruction to formality in every dimension $m\geqslant 2$. One monoid to realize them all: a master monoid for homotopy types

Isaac Carcac\'ia-Campos · 2611.32655
math.ATmath.CT
Abstract

Homotopy theory has traditionally been approached either through topological spaces or through categories of presheaves on suitable combinatorial categories, a perspective subsumed by Grothendieck's theory of test categories. In this paper, we develop a more algebraic viewpoint: homotopy types can be represented by actions of a single monoid on sets. This monoid is obtained by identifying all the objects of the semisimplicial category while retaining its face operators. Every CW homotopy type is the classifying space of the translation category of one of its actions, and its truncation at dimension n is finitely presented and realizes exactly the homotopy types of dimension at most n. The construction rests on a general result: every set-valued functor on a small category freely generates an action of its universal monoid, and its category of elements is a full coreflective subcategory of the resulting translation category. Hence every weak test category gives rise to a monoid whose actions realize all homotopy types; when the category has more than one object, however, this monoid is not itself a weak test category, so that a single monoid may realize all homotopy types without modelling homotopy theory as a localization. The obstruction lies in identifying objects, not in having a single one: there are strict test categories with a single object, such as the monoid of all self-maps of an infinite set and a countable monoid of primitive recursive functions.

Second homotopy modules and infinite homotopy stable classes of four-manifolds

Tommy Hofmann, John Nicholson · 2611.31642
math.GTmath.ATcross
Abstract

We construct infinitely many closed oriented smooth 4-manifolds which are all stably diffeomorphic but pairwise not homotopy equivalent. Our examples are distinguished by their second homotopy modules, and provide the first examples which demonstrate that stable diffeomorphism does not determine the second homotopy module. They have fundamental group $(\mathbb{Z}/4)^4*\mathbb{Z}$ and arise as doubles of presentation 2-complexes. In contrast, we give criteria for isomorphism of second homotopy modules of doubles of 2-complexes, covering finite abelian, quaternion, dihedral and symmetric fundamental groups. We also establish non-injectivity of the doubling construction by showing that there exist homotopically distinct presentation 2-complexes over $Q_{28}$ whose doubles are homotopy equivalent.

Topological Inference for degree-Cech complexes

Michael Kerber, Bria Razanaparany · 2611.31505
math.AT
Abstract

We study topological inference for the degree-Cech bifiltration from noisy samples of a manifold. Under a model combining uniform sampling on the manifold with uniform noise, we show that suitable choices of the radius and degree parameters recover the homotopy type of the underlying manifold with high probability.
