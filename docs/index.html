Derived plethystic algebra

Benjamin Antieau, Edith H\"ubner, Dmitry Kubrak, Joost Nuiten · 2610.12839
author: Benjamin Antieauabstract: infinity-categormath.AT
Abstract

We prove comparison results about $\infty$-categories of derived commutative rings with extra algebraic structure. For example, we show that several notions of derived $\delta$-rings agree and obtain similar results for derived $\lambda$-rings, derived binomial rings, and derived Boolean rings. Our comparison results apply more generally to modules over a specific kind of monad on derived rings that we call derived plethory.

Quillen's local parameter conjecture in dimension four

Yunpeng Li · 2610.13469
abstract: motivic cohomologabstract: algebraic k-theormath.AGmath.ACmath.KT
Abstract

Let $R$ be a Noetherian regular local ring of mixed characteristic and dimension four, and let $r$ be a member of a regular system of parameters. We prove that every finitely generated projective module over $R[1/r]$ is free. We also prove that $\mathrm{Sp}_4(R[1/r])$ acts transitively on unimodular columns of length four and that every determinant orientation of a rank-two projective module is induced by a basis. The argument reduces freeness to two vanishing results: the degree-three Zariski cohomology of the naive degree-four Milnor--Witt sheaf and the alternating Witt group of $R[1/r]$. We establish these results using integral degree-two algebraic $K$-theory, supported hermitian $K$-theory, and local motivic cohomology. A finite-rank symplectic comparison relates the image of the completion obstruction to Milnor--Witt multiplication and yields the required symplectic frames.

Reconstruction of graphs and surfaces from derived categories

Oscar Harr · 2610.13488
abstract: infinity-categorabstract: dualizable categormath.ATmath.KT
Abstract

A pair of topological spaces $X$ and $Y$ is a \emph{Fourier--Mukai (FM) pair} if there is an equivalence of $\infty$-categories of derived sheaves of abelian groups \[\operatorname{Shv}(X;\mathrm D(\mathbb Z))\simeq \operatorname{Shv}(Y;\mathrm D(\mathbb Z)).\] An FM pair $(X,Y)$ is \emph{non-trivial} if $X$ and $Y$ are not homeomorphic. We consider the question of whether non-trivial FM pairs $(X,Y)$ exist for which $X$ and $Y$ are both "nice" spaces, which in this article means admitting a triangulation. We show that the answer is no if $X$ is second-countable and either (i) one-dimensional, (ii) a surface, or (iii) the 3-sphere. In order to prove this, we define two invariants of dualizable categories. The first invariant is an extension of \emph{Tate--Hochschild cohomology} (aka singular Hochschild cohomology) to a class of dualizable categories satisfying a countability assumption. For a homology manifold $X$, we relate the Tate--Hochschild cohomology of $\operatorname{Shv}(X;\mathrm D(\mathbb Z))$ to the homology at infinity of $X$ (in the sense of proper homotopy theory). The second invariant takes the form of a subcategory $\mathcal D_{\mathrm{Gor}}\subseteq\mathcal D$ called the \emph{Gorenstein locus}, defined for every dualizable stable category $\mathcal D$. For a triangularizable space $X$ and a field $k$, we show that the Gorenstein locus of $\operatorname{Shv}(X;\mathrm D(k))$ is equal to the full subcategory of sheaves supported on the $k$-homology manifold locus of $X$.

Constructive Bounds for the Topological Expressivity of Neural Networks

Kosio Beshkov · 2610.13931
math.ATcs.LG
Abstract

The expressivity of a neural network $\Phi$ describes the class of functions that a network can express. The term topological expressivity refers to a family of measures particularly suited to understand binary classification and is often formulated in terms of the Betti numbers of a decision boundary $\beta (\Phi^{-1}(0))$. Despite its popularity, this measure is not as useful for regression and other problems that do not involve a decision boundary. Instead, we propose to study topological expressivity in terms of the Betti numbers of the output of a neural network $\beta(\Phi(X))$. We formally prove that a ReLU network with architecture $(d,\sum\limits_{k=1}^{d } \beta_k(3k+2),\sum\limits_{k=1}^{d}\beta_k(k+1))$ can express spaces homotopy equivalent to wedge sums of spheres with arbitrary Betti numbers. This gives upper bounds for any arbitrary connected ($\beta_0=1$) sequence of Betti numbers, but by constructing spaces homotopy equivalent to products of $N$ spheres of dimension $d_1,...,d_N$ we achieve much better bounds $(\sum\limits_{n=1} d_n, 3\sum\limits_{n=1}\limits^N d_n + 2N, \sum\limits_{n=1}\limits^N d_n + N) $ for Betti numbers given by $\prod_n (1+x^{d_n})$. Finally we conjecture a much stronger tight bound which gives the architectural bound $(d,d+1,d+1)$ for a space homeomorphic to the sphere.

Critical Point Theory for the Distance Function on PL-Spaces

Dirk Siersma · 2610.13297
math.ATmath.GT
Abstract

We study the (typical) singularities of the distance function from a point in the ambient space to a polytopal complex in Euclidean space. By means of the Goresky-MacPherson splitting we generalize the existing linear theory to the quadratic distance function. Critical points occur on `selected' strata of the PL-space, where normal Morse Data determine the change of topology. On the homology level there exist indices with multiplicities, combined in a Morse vector. We focus mainly on PL-manifolds. We give bounds, depending on the dimension of the strata. In low dimensions we describe singularity types. At the end we discuss relations with Discrete Morse Theory.

The (right) homotopy between two A$_{\infty}$-morphisms

Mattia Ornaghi · 2610.13803
math.ATmath.AGmath.CT
Abstract

Let $f$, $g\colon A\to B$ be two A$_{\infty}$-morphisms between two A$_{\infty}$-algebras (linear over a commutative ring $\mathbb{K}$). In this paper, we compare different notions of (right) homotopy between $f$ and $g$. We note that the A$_{\infty}$-algebra $B\otimes R$, where $R:=\mathbb{K}[t,dt]$, is a model for the interval $[0,1]\times B$ in the sense of Fukaya-Oh-Ohta-Ono, and we give a short alternative proof, in the unfiltered case, of the existence of comparison morphisms between such models. We use it to prove that two A$_{\infty}$-morphisms are homotopic \`a la Seidel if and only if, considered as morphisms of 2-colored DG operads, they are elementary homotopic \`a la Markl-Shnider-Stasheff through a homotopy which is constant on the two monochromatic parts. We also provide an example of two A$_{\infty}$-morphisms which are elementary homotopic but not weakly equivalent (and so homotopic \`a la Seidel). Moreover, over a field, we observe that the models for the interval are good path objects in the category of A$_{\infty}$-algebras with (non strict) A$_{\infty}$-morphisms, and we explain why weakly equivalent A$_{\infty}$-morphisms need not be homotopic.

The first homology of square-section braid groups

Minh Pham, Nicholas Wawrykow, Jacob Zweiback · 2610.12633
math.AT
Abstract

We compute the first homology of the unordered configuration space of $n$ open unit-squares in the $w$ by $h$ rectangle for all positive integers $n$, $w$, and $h$. Letting $w$ and $h$ vary, these square configuration spaces form a bi-filtration of the unordered configuration space of $n$ points in the plane, so their fundamental groups approximate the $n$-strand braid group. Using a pair of spectral sequences, we prove that these square and point configuration spaces have naturally isomorphic first homology groups if $w,h\ge 3$ and $n\le wh-3$. In the $h=2$ case, we show that the first homology groups of these square configuration spaces are free of rank $n-1$ for $n\le 2w-2$, whereas they are free of rank $w-1$ for $n=2w-1$.

Weak and genuine A_n-formality for number fields

Ambrus P\'al, Gereon Quick · 2610.11390
math.NTmath.ATmath.GRcross
Abstract

We introduce weak $A_n$-formality for Galois cochain algebras and, for profinite groups whose mod-$p$ cohomology is concentrated in degrees at most two, characterise weak and genuine $A_n$-formality through finite embedding problems and the projective system they form. We then study $A_n$-formality for number fields. Let $K$ be a number field and let $p$ be an odd prime with $\mu_p\not\subset K$. For $p\geq 5$, and for $p=3$ under a natural local cyclotomic condition, we prove that every associated finite $A_3$-embedding problem is solvable. In contrast, for every odd $p$ with $\mu_p\not\subset K$, we show that no compatible family of finite-stage solutions exists, and hence $C^\bullet(G_K,\mathbb F_p)$ is not genuinely $A_3$-formal. The proof uses Chebotarev density, governing fields, and the Gras-Munnier criterion to obstruct the compatibility of finite-stage solutions, and it builds on previous work of Maire-Min\'a\v{c}-Ramakrishna-T\^an. This shows that genuine $A_3$-formality detects a global compatibility obstruction for number fields invisible to every individual finite embedding problem and invisible to obstructions arising from individual strong Massey vanishing problems.
