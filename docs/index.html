On the Adams spectral sequence of $eo_2(C_4)$

Christian Carrick · 2611.16531
author: Christian Carricktitle: adams spectral sequencetitle: spectral sequenceabstract: k(2)-localabstract: comodulemath.AT
Abstract

We calculate the mod $2$-homology of the fixed points of the $C_4$-equivariant Hill--Hopkins--Ravenel theory $BP^{((C_4))}\langle 1\rangle$, as an $\mathcal{A}_*$-comodule algebra. $2$-locally these fixed points are $tmf_0(5)$ by a theorem of Quinn. We deduce that $tmf_0(5)$ is an indecomposable $tmf$-module that is an extension of two $tmf$-modules of the form $tmf\otimes K$ for a finite spectrum $K$. The $K(2)$-localization of $tmf_0(5)$ is $32$-periodic, and we compute the Adams spectral sequence of $tmf_0(5)$ through the $32$-stem.

Higher Categorical Bistellar Moves

Aleksandar Ivanov · 2611.17244
title: higher categormath.CThep-thmath-phmath.MP
Abstract

We construct $n$-categories $\mathscr{P}_n$ with generators given by simplices and relations given by bistellar moves between them, in any dimension $n$. We show that functors out of $\mathscr{P}_n$ into an $n$-category of theories, give the correct input for the orbifold construction for TQFTs, and subsume all previously known examples, which are all in dimensions $n \leq 3$. In particular, in dimension 3 the construction gives a highly nontrivial categorification of a kind of nonunital, noncounital, Frobenius algebra.

Lax Centers and Monoidal Functors for Comodule Categories Associated with Acyclic Quivers

Wentao Zhao, Jingheng Zhou · 2611.15496
title: comodulemath.RTmath.RA
Abstract

In this paper, we study the monoidal category of finite-dimensional right comodules over the path algebra $\Bbbk Q$ of a finite acyclic quiver $Q$, equipped with the weak bialgebra structure in which every path is grouplike. We prove that the lax center of this category is monoidally equivalent to $\operatorname{Rep}^{\mathrm{fd}}_{\Bbbk}(Q)$, the category of finite-dimensional representations of $Q$. We also classify all $\Bbbk$-linear strong monoidal functors between any two such comodule categories, and all monoidal natural transformations between them. For the right adjoint of such a functor, we determine when the left or right projection formula holds. When both projection formulas hold, this adjoint will induce a functor between the lax centers, and we describe the induced functor explicitly on quiver representations.

Maximal nilpotence for higher Verlinde categories

Kevin Coulembier, Johannes Flake, Joseph Newton, Victor Ostrik · 2611.14810
title: nilpotenmath.RTmath.CT
Abstract

We show that all higher Verlinde categories are maximally nilpotent, as conjectured by Coulembier (2023). By previous results, it suffices to show that non-trivial simple objects have only finitely many non-zero symmetric powers in odd characteristic. We compute the maximal degrees of these non-zero symmetric and exterior powers explicitly, hence confirming their finiteness. We discuss some of the far-reaching consequences.

Representation Theory and the Algebraic K-Theory of Geometric Fields

Gunnar Carlsson, Roy Joshua · 2611.15605
title: algebraic k-theorabstract: ring spectrmath.KTmath.AT
Abstract

We apply a derived completion construction for commutative ring spectra to give a description of the K-theory spectrum of a geometric field F (i.e. containing an algebraically closed subfield $k$) of characteristic zero purely in terms of the representation theory of its absolute Galois group $G_F$. A result that follows is that the l-adically completed Milnor K-theory of F is isomorphic to the derived completion of the representation ring of $G_F$. The main result, from which this follows, is that $K(F)$ completed at l is equivalent to the derived completion of the K-theory of the category of finite dimensional representations of $G_F$ in $k$.

On Tunnel Fusion and Yangian $R$-matrices

Dmitry Galakhov · 2611.14793
abstract: stable envelopehep-thmath-phmath.AGmath.MPcross
Abstract

XXX spin-chain Hamiltonians can be derived within the $R$-matrix formalism. For a simple Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, the rational $R$-matrix $R$ intertwines the coproduct $\Delta$ in the Yangian $\mathsf{Y}(\mathfrak{g})$. $R$ admits a particular Gauss decomposition $R=LDU$, where the upper-triangular matrix $U$ can be obtained from the lower-triangular matrix $L$. We treat $U$ in three ways. Algebraically, $U$ is known as a factorization twist. It plays the role of a ``universalizer'': it conjugates a non-universal pseudo-coproduct $\mathscr{D}$ in the molten crystal chain basis to the true Yangian coproduct $\Delta=U^{-1}\mathscr{D} U$. Physically, $U$ can be obtained as a Berry phase in SUSY quiver QFT for a quiver associated with the Dynkin diagram of $\mathfrak{g}$. In this case, $U$ is calculated as a sum over tunneling amplitudes. Geometrically, $U$ can be interpreted as a renormalized Maulik-Okounkov stable envelope localized at a fixed point. In the physical setting, the Yang-Baxter equation (YBE), which ensures integrability, follows from the flatness of the Berry connection, which in turn follows from supersymmetry. However, the YBE imposes subtle constraints on the tunneling amplitudes, which we examine here. We construct recursion relations that allow higher collective amplitudes to be obtained from more elementary ones. We observe that different tunneling channels are in fact intertwined, leading to finer cancellations among amplitudes and to wall-crossing phenomena: depending on the parameters, the same amplitude is represented as a sum over different channels. We focus on $\mathsf{Y}(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$ with higher-spin and non-evaluation representations, as well as on the fundamental representation of $\mathsf{Y}(\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1})$.

Asymptotic Dimension of Persistence Modules of Bounded Presentation

Sushovan Majhi, Atish Mitra, \v{Z}iga Virk · 2611.16965
math.MGcs.CGmath.ATcross
Abstract

We compute the asymptotic dimension of spaces of multiparameter persistence modules of bounded presentation under the interleaving distance. The $d$-parameter modules with at most $n$ generators and $m$ relations have asymptotic dimension, and Assouad-Nagata dimension, exactly $d(n+m)$, the number of coordinates of their generator and relation grades, over every field; for two parameters this is $2(n+m)$. The space has continuous moduli, already for two parameters and $n,m\ge2$: over an infinite field it contains, at every scale, infinite uniformly separated families of pairwise non-isomorphic modules with one fibered barcode, so it is not doubling even at a fixed fibered barcode; already for $m\ge1$ it is not coarsely equivalent to a proper metric space. The proof is an induction on the number of distinct grade coordinates: an interleaving small compared with the gaps between these coordinates moves each of them by at most twice its size and preserves the induced grid representation, so the continuous moduli are invisible at large scale. Each class of modules at finite distance therefore embeds coarsely into Hilbert space; for $n,m\ge2$ over an uncountable field, the classes that contain these families admit no such embedding into a separable Hilbert space. Under the two-parameter matching distance $2(n+m)$ is a lower bound, attained for one generator and for two generators with at most one relation; on a finite net of $S$ slices the asymptotic dimension is at most $2nS$.

Chern characteristic classes of normal bundles of complex generalized flag submanifolds in generalized flag manifolds

Jakub Knesel, Petr Somberg · 2611.16837
math.AGmath.ATmath.DGmath.RT
Abstract

We study Chern characteristic classes of normal vector bundles of complex generalized flag submanifolds $G_{n-1}/P_{n-1}$ in complex generalized flag manifolds $G_n/P_n$. This procedure can be regarded as the relative version of Schubert calculus for the pair $G_{n-1}/P_{n-1}$ and $G_n/P_n$. The CW structure and additive structure of the cohomology of $\mathrm{Gr}(n, k)$ allows inductive calculation of Chern classes of the canonical and complementary canonical vector bundles over $\mathrm{Gr}(n, k)$. The combination of representation theory and algebraic geometry allows to derive analogous properties for the isotropic Grassmannians $\mathrm{LGr}(m, k)$ and $\mathrm{OGr}(m, k)$ via their embeddings in $\mathrm{Gr}(m, k)$. Then the induced CW structure yields the additive structure on the cohomology of $\mathrm{LGr}(m, k)$ and $\mathrm{OGr}(m, k)$, and consequently leads to the calculation of Chern classes of the canonical and complementary canonical vector bundles over $\mathrm{LGr}(m, k)$ and $\mathrm{OGr}(m, k)$.

Gluing Geometric Chains Of Manifolds And Orbifolds With Corners

Hao Yu · 2611.15551
math.GTmath-phmath.ATmath.MPcross
Abstract

Geometric chains are formal combinations of smooth maps from compact oriented manifolds or orbifolds with corners. In earlier work the author showed that, modulo a relation D that kills degenerate chains, they compute rational singular homology. We show that D can be replaced by a gluing relation, which identifies a chain with the sum of its restrictions to finitely many pieces whenever its source is glued from these pieces along whole codimension-one faces. With this relation the comparison map from singular homology is an isomorphism in all degrees for compact polyhedra and smooth manifolds without boundary, with rational and with integer coefficients. Unlike D, the gluing relation is defined through the sources alone, and chain-level identities such as the equivalence of a degree d covering with d times its base, which fails modulo D, and the correction terms of the chain map from a Morse complex, which are degenerate modulo D, become gluing relations. The proof is axiomatic; injectivity comes from integration or dual cells, surjectivity from cutting along regular levels, where the cut of a gluing relation is again one, and at the point we use Whitehead triangulations of manifolds with corners subordinate to their corner strata. The gluing relation, read on underlying spaces, also suffices for orbifold sources, with integer coefficients modulo classes with an orientation reversing automorphism, and for effective orbifold targets.

Lagrangian cobordisms and morphisms of persistence modules

math.SGmath.ATcross
Abstract

We consider a version of Legendrian contact homology filtered by the action of Reeb chords. In this version, a union of two disjoint exact Lagrangian cobordisms with Legendrian boundary conditions gives rise to a morphism between persistence modules. Path complexes with concatenation and their relation to digraphs

Yuri Muranov, Anna Muranova · 2611.15308
math.AT
Abstract

The path homology theory is based on the notion of path complex and is a part of GLMY-theory created and developed by Grigor'yan, Lin, Muranov, and Yau in a cycle of papers in 2012-2014. In this paper we define a path complex with concatenation ($PC$-complex) which is a particular case of a path complex. We define a natural topology on a set of vertices of any $PC$-complex, and describe properties of this topology and its relation to the discrete topology of Alexandroff. We prove the existence of functorial relations between some categories of $PC$-complexes and digraphs. These relations allow to transfer covering, homotopy and various versions of homology theory from digraph theory to $PC$-complexes.

Wasserstein Stability of the Nerve Map

Kenneth McCabe, Jose A. Perea · 2611.15938
math.ATmath.MG
Abstract

Given a locally finite open cover of a paracompact space, a subordinate partition of unity defines a map into the geometric realization of the nerve. When the cover is good, the nerve theorem asserts that this map is a homotopy equivalence. In applied topology, one specializes this construction to covers of metric data by open balls centered at finitely many landmarks. The corresponding nerve map represents the data inside the \v{C}ech complex, enabling one to pull cohomological or spectral invariants back to the data. Since point cloud data often comes equipped with weights encoding domain-specific importance, we introduce a weighted version of the nerve map and study its stability under perturbations of the landmarks and weights using metrics from optimal transport. Our main result is a Lipschitz stability theorem for the weighted nerve map under a uniform nondegeneracy assumption. We state the bound in terms of a scale-dependent optimal transport distance, and give an example where our estimate is attained. Since this distance is dominated by a multiple of the $1$-Wasserstein distance, Wasserstein stability follows as a corollary. We also give geometric conditions ensuring nondegeneracy, obtain explicit estimates of the Lipschitz constant for closed Riemannian manifolds, and sketch applications to Laplacian eigenvector cascading and Eilenberg--MacLane coordinates.
