author: Tom Bachmannauthor: Michael Hopkinstitle: motivicabstract: stable stemsabstract: motivic steenrodmath.AGmath.ATmath.KT
Abstract

We provide a novel, homotopy theoretic model for the motivic Eilenberg--Mac Lane spaces $K(Z_{(p)}(n), 2n) \in Spc(k)$ over fields of characteristic p. Using this, we determine their motives and, consequently, determine the motivic Steenrod algebra at the characteristic. The Derived Auslander--Iyama Correspondence II: Bimodule Calabi--Yau Structures

Gustavo Jasso, Fernando Muro · 2509.22625
author: Gustavo Jassomath.RTreplace
Abstract

Let $d$ be a positive integer. In a previous article we established a bijective correspondence between the following classes of objects, considered up to the appropriate notion of equivalence: differential graded algebras (dg) with finite-dimensional $0$-th cohomology such that the canonical generator of their perfect derived category is a basic $d\ZZ$-cluster tilting object, and basic Frobenius algebras that are twisted $(d+2)$-periodic as bimodules. In this article, we prove a variant of our general correspondence for bimodule right Calabi--Yau dg algebras. Unfolding of symmetric monoidal $(\infty,n)$-categories

Rune Haugseng, Thomas Nikolaus · 2611.01994
author: Rune Haugsengauthor: Thomas Nikolaustitle: (infinity,n)abstract: infinity-categorabstract: algebraic k-theormath.ATmath.CTmath.KT
Abstract

We prove Lurie's proposed description of left rigid symmetric monoidal $(\infty,n)$-categories in terms of purely $\infty$-categorical data. More precisely, unfolding gives an equivalence between left rigid symmetric monoidal $(\infty,n)$-categories and complete categorical chain complexes of rigid symmetric monoidal $\infty$-categories. More generally, unfolding identifies skeletal sequences with categorical chain complexes without the completeness condition. We extend these equivalences to $(\infty,\infty)$-categories and, stably, obtain an equivalence between complete unbounded categorical chain complexes and left rigid categorical spectra. We also analyze their groupoid completion and express the homotopy groups of the associated spaces and spectra in terms of categorical cycles and boundaries. As examples, we construct the categorical chain complexes associated to geometric and algebraic bordism categories, including their equivariant variants, and to iterated span categories. Their localizations recover classical bordism and $L$-groups, while the span examples are related to algebraic $K$-theory.

Mohammadali Aligholi · 2611.02577

Abstract

For a compact oriented even-dimensional Witt pseudomanifold $X$, we compute the $S_n$-equivariant Banagl--Zagier $L$-classes of $X^n$ in terms of the Goresky--MacPherson $L$-classes of $X$ and the intersection cohomology Euler characteristic of $X$. Absolute descent and the B\'enabou--Roubaud theorem

Fernando Lucatelli Nunes · 2611.01654
title: descentmath.CTmath.AG
Abstract

We continue the programme developed in Pseudo-Kan extensions and descent theory by studying descent for augmented pseudocosimplicial objects. Let $\mathcal H$ be a $2$-category, and let $\operatorname{suc}=\mathsf{1}+(-)$ be the successor monad on the augmented simplex category. Precomposition with $\operatorname{suc}$ induces the d\'ecalage pseudomonad $\mathbb S$ on the $2$-category of augmented pseudocosimplicial objects with values in $\mathcal H$. We prove that every successor contraction makes the corresponding augmented pseudocosimplicial object effective for descent. This structure is preserved by postcomposition with every pseudofunctor. Consequently, the resulting descent object is an absolute bilimit. A first application concerns split epimorphisms. We recover the Janelidze--Tholen characterization of absolute effective descent morphisms given in Facets of Descent II. Moreover, we prove that one direction holds for every pseudofunctor $A\colon C^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal H$, where $\mathcal H$ is an arbitrary $2$-category. Consequently, every $\mathbb S$-pseudoalgebra is of absolute descent. In particular, this holds for every free $\mathbb S$-pseudoalgebra, and hence for every one-sided d\'ecalage. This makes the shift-effectiveness hypothesis in the B\'enabou--Roubaud theorem of Pseudo-Kan extensions and descent theory automatic. Thus, under the adjointness and Beck--Chevalley hypotheses, the descent and Eilenberg--Moore factorizations are equivalent whenever they exist. We thus obtain a stronger version of the B\'enabou--Roubaud theorem for arbitrary augmented pseudocosimplicial objects, which we call the formal B\'enabou--Roubaud theorem. Applied to indexed \v{C}ech objects associated with pseudofunctors $A\colon C^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal H$, this theorem extends the classical B\'enabou--Roubaud theorem to an arbitrary $2$-category $\mathcal H$.

Koszul duality for boundary categories

Yuan Gao, Zhen Gao · 2611.02080
title: koszul dualmath.KTmath.CTmath.RT
Abstract

We study how Koszul duality relates two kinds of boundary constructions associated with a smooth category and a proper category. For a smooth category with a proper Koszul-dualizing subcategory, we construct a fully faithful functor from its categorical neighborhood of infinity to the singularity category associated to the proper category. We prove that this functor is an equivalence precisely when the smooth category is reflexive, meaning that it can be reconstructed from its finite-dimensional module theory. We extend the definition of cdh-sheafified Hochschild homology from varieties to stable categories linear over a field of characteristic zero, thereby proving its Morita-invariance. On the representability of actions of algebras with several bilinear multiplications

Manuel Mancini · 2611.02097
abstract: presentablemath.CTmath.RA
Abstract

We study the representability of actions in varieties of algebras with $n \geq 1$ not necessarily associative bilinear multiplications. We first prove that, for homogeneous varieties, action accessibility is equivalent to being an Orzech category of interest. For an action accessible, operadic variety we then construct the external weak actor $\mathcal{E}(X)$ of an algebra $X$, an algebra with $n$ bilinear partial operations together with a natural monomorphism encoding the actions on $X$. We characterise the algebras admitting an actor in terms of $\mathcal{E}(X)$, and we deduce an obstruction to action representability: if, for some algebra $X$ with trivial multiplications, $\mathcal{E}(X)$ does not satisfy a multilinear identity of the variety, then the variety is not action representable. Computing the external weak actor explicitly, we obtain that associative and matching dialgebras are weakly action representable but not action representable, and that Poisson algebras, left-right non-commutative Poisson algebras and matching Lie algebras are not action representable. Finally, we introduce the reduced type $(p,q)$ of such a variety and prove that, over a field of characteristic different from $2$, an operadic variety of reduced type $(p,0)$, $(0,q)$ or $(1,1)$ is action representable if and only if it is term equivalent to the variety of Lie algebras or to that of abelian algebras. For the reduced types $(p,0)$ and $(0,q)$, the arguments require no assumption on the cardinality of the field, whereas the case $(1,1)$ requires the field to be infinite.

A Directed Cubical Framework for k-critical Bipersistence Modules

Robin Belton, Robyn Brooks, Lisbeth Fajstrup, Brittany Terese Fasy, Elizabeth Vidaurre · 2611.00261
math.AT
Abstract

In topological data analysis, multiparameter persistence modules (MPMs) are used to analyze data with multiple parameters. Summarizing topological information for MPMs is a non-trivial task; one useful summary of bipersistence modules is the fibered barcode \cite{lesnick2015interactive,lesnick2025fast}. In this paper, we use a directed topological construction of the parameter space of a bifiltration to extend the fibered barcode. Specifically, given a $k$-critical bifiltration $F$, we build an associated directed Euclidean cubical complex $C$ whose directed paths each correspond to a one-parameter subfiltration of $F$. The path space of $C$, $\di{C}$, is used to define the \emph{extended fibered barcode}, which contains all possible monotonically increasing paths in $C$. We show that there can be paths in $\di{C}$ which generate filtrations not obtainable along a line with positive slope, and that all lines with positive slope have a path in $\di{C}$ that generate an equivalent barcode. We introduce two types of equivalence for these restricted modules: combinatorial equivalence of diagrams, and simplicial equivalence of the filtrations generating the modules. We show that removing certain cubes from $C$ yields a subcomplex on which dihomotopic paths induce simplicially equivalent filtrations, and hence combinatorially equivalent diagrams.

Abstract

Determining the representative volume element is essential for establishing statistically meaningful microstructure-property relationships. Conventional approaches rely on the convergence of selected geometric descriptors, which may yield different estimates of the representative volume. Here, we propose a topology-based framework for assessing the representative scale of a nickel-yttria-stabilized zirconia solid oxide cell anode reconstructed using plasma focused ion beam-scanning electron microscopy (121.8 um x 83.97 um x 45.99 um). Cohomology of regular spherical actions

Jos\'e Ignacio Royo Prieto, Martintxo Saralegi-Aranguren, Robert Wolak · 2611.02111
math.AT
Abstract

We study regular spherical actions, namely, actions of a connected compact Lie group G on a manifold M with equidimensional orbits, some of which are spheres. These spherical orbits are diffeomorphic to homogeneous spaces G/H, and there is a list of nine possible families of pairs (G, H). The normalizer N of H in G plays an important role in our calculations, as does the Weyl group W = N/H. In fact, there are three possibilities for W, namely: Z_2, S^1 and S^3. The goal of this paper is to relate the cohomology of M to that of the orbit space M/G. We obtain a particularly simple relationship in the first case, W = Z_2. For the remaining two cases, W = S^1 and W = S^3, it is already known that the cohomology of M and that of the orbit space M/G are related by the Gysin sequence ... \to H^k(M/G) \to H^k(M ) \to H^{k-d}(M/G) \to H^{k+1}(M/G) \to ... In this work, we provide a direct construction of this sequence, avoiding the use of spectral sequences. Moreover, we give an explicit description of the connecting homomorphism as multiplication by a power of the Euler class associated with the action of the Weyl group W = S^l on the fixed-point set M^H.

Abstract

We define generalized Bernoulli numbers as appropriate coefficients of the Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff (BCH) product formula, extending the classical sequence of rational numbers to a 2-dimensional setting. We give an identity involving these numbers and the classical Bernoulli numbers which is equivalent to the existence of a particular complete differential graded Lie algebra modelling a cell decomposition of the 2-disk and based on the Lawrence--Sullivan model of the interval.
