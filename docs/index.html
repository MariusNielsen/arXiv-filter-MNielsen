Atiyah duality for motivic spectra

Toni Annala, Marc Hoyois, Ryomei Iwasa · 2403.01561
author: Marc Hoyoistitle: motivic spectrtitle: motivicabstract: moduli stack of formal groupsabstract: infinity-categorabstract: algebraic k-theormath.AGmath.ATmath.KTreplace
Abstract

We prove that Atiyah duality holds in the $\infty$-category of non-$\mathbb A^1$-invariant motivic spectra over arbitrary derived schemes: every smooth projective scheme is dualizable with dual given by the Thom spectrum of its negative tangent bundle. The Gysin maps recently constructed by L. Tang are a key ingredient in the proof. We then present several applications. First, we study $\mathbb A^1$-colocalization, which transforms any module over the $\mathbb A^1$-invariant sphere into an $\mathbb A^1$-invariant motivic spectrum without changing its values on smooth projective schemes. This can be applied to all known $p$-adic cohomology theories and gives a new elementary approach to "logarithmic" or "tame" cohomology theories; it recovers for instance the logarithmic crystalline cohomology of strict normal crossings compactifications over perfect fields and shows that the latter is independent of the choice of compactification. Second, we prove a motivic Landweber exact functor theorem, associating a motivic spectrum to any graded formal group law classified by a flat map to the moduli stack of formal groups. Using this theorem, we compute the ring of $\mathbb P^1$-stable cohomology operations on the algebraic K-theory of qcqs derived schemes, and we prove that rational motivic cohomology is an idempotent motivic spectrum.

Relative Langlands duality for linear periods

Tsao-Hsien Chen, Sanath K. Devalapurkar, Mark Macerato, David Nadler · 2610.31771
author: Sanath K. Devalapurkarmath.RTmath.AG
Abstract

We prove relative Langlands duality for $X = GL_{p+q}/(GL_p \times GL_q)$. These symmetric spaces arise in the setting of the linear period of Friedberg-Jacquet when $p=q$, and exhibit non-tempered behavior when $q>p+1$. Via the real-symmetric correspondence, we establish a derived Satake equivalence for the real group $G_{\mathbb R}=U(p,q)$, and use it to obtain a constructible realization of coherent sheaves on Kato's exotic nilpotent cones. Along the way, we describe the dual variety of $X$ in terms of the Whittaker cotangent bundle and regular centralizers, viewed as Coulomb branches.

Deformations of cohomology classes and spectral sequences

Borislav Mladenov · 2610.32593
title: spectral sequencemath.AGmath.ATmath.RT
Abstract

We study spectral sequences as an obstruction theory to deforming cohomology classes via the Rees construction. A filtered complex is interpreted as an equivariant family over the affine line, in which higher spectral sequence differentials arise as obstructions to lifting cohomology classes through infinitesimal neighborhoods of the special fibre. This leads to various geometric and module-theoretic degeneration criteria. We give various applications to $\mathrm{Ext}$ spectral sequences for complex Lagrangians and the formality results of Melani-Rubi\'o amongst others. For double complexes, we revisit the Khovanov-Qi decomposition on the Rees side and introduce a two-parameter Rees construction over the affine plane. The higher differentials of the two associated spectral sequences correspond to torsion modules supported on the thickened coordinate axes. We obtain degeneration criteria in terms of these modules and an intrinsic characterisation of the $\partial\bar\partial$-lemma.

Angle cocycles, Euler characteristic, and simplicial volume of smooth manifolds

Michelle Bucher, Stefano Francaviglia, Grazia Rago · 2610.32342
abstract: straighteningmath.GTmath.ATcross
Abstract

A celebrated conjecture of Gromov states that for closed aspherical manifolds, the vanishing of simplicial volume implies the vanishing of the Euler characteristic. We prove Gromov's conjecture for closed convex projective manifolds and, more generally, for closed orientable smooth manifolds admitting an immersed straightening. Generalising the straightening of simplices in properly convex domains in real projective space, we introduce the notion of immersed straightening and prove that, for every integer $d\geq 1$, there exists a constant $C_d>0$ such that every closed orientable smooth $d$-manifold $M$ admitting an immersed straightening satisfies $|\chi(M)|\le C_d||M||$.

Kashiwara's Lemma for Potent D-modules

Jacob Erlikhman, Kendric Schefers · 2610.32003
abstract: genuine equivariantmath.AGmath.ATmath.RT
Abstract

On stacks there is a richer version of $\mathcal{D}$-modules---called $\textit{potent }\mathcal{D}\textit{-modules}$---which decompletes at equivariant parameters, in the same sense that genuine equivariant $K$-theory decompletes equivariant cohomology. In this paper, we prove a ``genuine'' version of Kashiwara's lemma for $\mathcal{D}$-modules. Namely, we show that for a broad class of geometric stacks $X$, if $Z \subset X$ is a closed substack, the category $\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{pot}}(Z)$ of potent $\mathcal{D}$-modules on $Z$ is equivalent under pushforward to the category $\mathcal{D}^{\mathrm{pot}} _Z(X)$ of potent $\mathcal{D}$-modules on $X$ with support contained in $Z$.

Associative Lifts of CW Semigroups

Stefano Bonzio, Andrea Loi · 2610.32426
math.ATmath.GNmath.GR
Abstract

Every nonempty connected finite-dimensional CW topological semigroup admits a continuous associative multiplication on its universal cover that lifts the multiplication of the base. Such a multiplication is unique up to a deck transformation: in fact, any two associative lifts are conjugate whenever they exist. We show that the finite-dimensional hypothesis cannot be dropped in general: a connected, countable, infinite-dimensional CW topological semigroup is constructed for which continuous lifts of multiplication exist, but none is associative. The counterexample has nonabelian fundamental group isomorphic to Thompson's group $F$. Thus finite dimensionality is sufficient for associative lifting in the CW setting, whereas the unrestricted infinite-dimensional statement fails.

Presheaf structures governed by oriented matroids and pseudohyperplane arrangements

Basile Coron, Denis Lyskov · 2610.31837
math.ATmath.CO
Abstract

We study higher complements of pseudohyperplane arrangements and the specialization maps between them, induced by degenerations. We develop a presheaf formalism on oriented matroids and weak maps, which model degenerations. Our main constructions are the posets of weighted and weightwise topes, which provide functorial combinatorial models for higher complements and their specialization morphisms. Their presheaf structure yields a deletion-contraction homotopy pushout, from which we derive a uniform combinatorial proof of the integral cohomology presentation for all $n \geq 2$. This presentation is natural with respect to weak maps and identifies cohomology rings with higher Orlik-Solomon algebras extending the known formulas for linear arrangements to non-realizable oriented matroids. Finally, we equip Deligne groupoids with a presheaf structure and identify them functorially with the fundamental groupoids of the complexified complements.

Realizable Cohomology Classes of Dihedral and Generalized Quaternion Groups

Sutirtha Datta, Jyotirmoy Ganguly, Rohit Joshi · 2610.32680
math.RTmath.ATmath.GR
Abstract

We compute the Chern classes of irreducible representations for the dihedral groups $D_{2n}$ and the generalized quaternion groups $Q_{2^n}$. Utilizing these computations, we investigate whether every even-dimensional class in the integral group cohomology of these families can be realized as a Chern class of some complex representation. It turns out that this realizability question answers in the affirmative for the generalized quaternion groups. In contrast, it turns out that the answer for dihedral groups is erratic.

The number of Tverberg partitions beyond prime powers

Matija Blagojevi\'c, Pavle V. M. Blagojevi\'c · 2610.32086
math.COmath.ATmath.MGcross
Abstract

Sierksma's conjecture asserts that every affine map $a\colon\Delta_{(d+1)(r-1)}\longrightarrow\mathbb{R}^d$ has at least $((r-1)!)^d$ unordered Tverberg partitions into $r$ parts. Until now, lower bounds growing with $d$ were known only when $r$ is a prime power. As one of our main results we prove that for every integer $r\geq3$, every decomposition $r=kq+s$ into $q=p^\alpha$ a prime power, $k\geq1$ and $s\geq0$, and every affine map $a$, the number $\operatorname{Tv}(a)$ of unordered Tverberg partitions satisfies \[ \operatorname{Tv}(a)\ \geq\ \frac{q}{r!}\,\Big(\frac{q}{\alpha+1}\Big)^{\big\lceil\frac{(r-1-2s)(d+1)}{2}\big\rceil}. \]
