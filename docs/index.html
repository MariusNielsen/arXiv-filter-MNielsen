Exact, total and filtered small categories

Floris Alderliefste · 2611.01026
math.CTmath.ATmath.COHexagonAI-generated (no human author claimed)
Abstract

For a non-empty connected small category we compare four properties: being filtered; exactness of colimits of abelian groups; Grothendieck's total asphericity, which says that colimits of simplicial sets over the category are homotopy colimits; and being quasi-filtered, which means that two arrows with a common source can be completed to a commutative square and that two parallel arrows are equalized by an integral combination of arrows whose coefficients sum to 1. Each implies the next. We give a category of finite ordered hypergraphs that is totally aspherical and whose colimits are exact over no non-zero ring, and we show that the category of finite ordered two-graphs (sets of triples that meet every four-element set in an even number of triples) is quasi-filtered and not totally aspherical. With the example of Isbell and Mitchell, the three implications are strict. A conjecture in Grothendieck's Les Dérivateurs, that totally aspherical categories are filtered, is therefore false; it holds for sifted categories, those over which colimits of sets commute with finite products.

The profinite Grothendieck-Teichmüller monoid is a group, on a question of Lochak and Schneps

Floris Alderliefste · 2611.01024
math.GRmath.ATmath.NTmath.AGHexagonAI-generated (no human author claimed)
Abstract

An element of the profinite Grothendieck-Teichmüller group $\widehat{\mathrm{GT}}$ of Drinfeld is a pair $(\lambda,f)$, with $\lambda$ a unit of $\widehat{\mathbb Z}$ and $f$ in the free profinite group $\widehat F_2$ on $x$ and $y$, that satisfies three relations and for which $x\mapsto x^\lambda$, $y\mapsto f^{-1}y^\lambda f$ is an automorphism of $\widehat F_2$. Lochak and Schneps asked in 2006 whether this last requirement follows from the others, and whether it holds with no relation at all (Question 1.1 of their list of open problems). We prove that it follows, and from less: if $\lambda\in1+2\widehat{\mathbb Z}$ and $f\in\widehat F_2$ satisfy the two-cycle and three-cycle relations (I) and (II), then $\lambda$ is a unit and the endomorphism is an automorphism. The pentagon relation is not used. Relation (I) alone does not suffice: for $\lambda=1$ and $f=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ the endomorphism is not surjective. It follows that the Grothendieck-Teichmüller monoid, in the profinite form of Horel and of Boavida de Brito, Horel and Robertson, is equal to its group of units; that every endomorphism of the profinite operad of parenthesized braids that fixes the objects is an automorphism; and, by their theorems, that every homotopy endomorphism of the profinite completion of the little 2-discs operad, or of the genus zero surface operad, is a homotopy automorphism. The proof counts boundary components of finite covers in homology. An endomorphism of the profinite completion of the fundamental group of a compact surface with boundary that sends every boundary generator to a conjugate of a unit power of a boundary generator, bijectively on boundary components, is an automorphism; on a sphere with at least three holes the exponents of such an endomorphism are equal, and are zero or a unit.
