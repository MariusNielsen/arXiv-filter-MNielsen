Equivariant localizing motives and multiplicative norms on algebraic K-theory

Kaif Hilman, Maxime Ramzi · 2603.15873
author: Maxime Ramzititle: algebraic k-theortitle: equivariantabstract: topological hochschild homologyabstract: thhabstract: genuine equivariantabstract: g-spectrmath.KTmath.ATreplace
Abstract

We construct multiplicative norms on equivariant nonconnective algebraic $K$-theory for finite groups $G$. We also construct a genuine equivariant version of THH equipped with a Dennis trace map from K-theory compatible with the multiplicative norms. To do so, we follow the general strategy of Blumberg-Gepner-Tabuada in the nonequivariant case by generalizing their category of localizing motives to the genuine equivariant context, building upon the theory of perfect $G$-stable categories of the first-named author. Crucially, we proceed using the recent perspective on noncommutative motives by the second-named author with Sosnilo and Winges which allows us to deal with non-exact functors on this category of motives. Together with an isotropy separation argument for equivariant cubes, we prove our main theorem that norms of stable categories preserve equivariant motivic equivalences. As an immediate consequence, we obtain a unique equivariant multiplicative refinement of nonconnective algebraic $K$-theory. From these constructions and results, we draw several applications, namely: (1) that the endofunctor of (equivariant) tensor powers on ordinary perfect stable categories preserve motivic equivalences; (2) that the multiplicative norms also preserve the additive motivic equivalences, thus yielding a motivic refinement of a result of Elmanto-Haugseng and Cnossen-Haugseng-Lenz-Linskens that connective algebraic K-theory admits multiplicative norms; (3) we construct a genuine equivariant version of topological Hochschild homology equipped with a Dennis trace map that is compatible with multiplicative norms; and (4) we prove that every genuine $G$-spectrum is the K-theory of a perfect $G$-stable category.

Parametrised functor calculus: excision, spheres, and semiadditivity

Kaif Hilman, Sil Linskens · 2410.22327
author: Sil Linskensabstract: goodwilliemath.ATreplace
Abstract

We lay down the foundations of a theory of parametrised functor calculus, generalising parts of the functor calculus of Goodwillie. We introduce the notion of excisable posets and develop a theory of excisive approximations in this context. As an application, we introduce two different excisable posets when parametrising over an atomic orbital category. By comparing the notions of excisiveness for these two posets, we relate the invertibility of certain spheres with Nardin's notion of parametrised semiadditivity, generalising Wirthm\"uller's classical result in equivariant homotopy theory for finite groups.

Involutions on $(\infty,n)$-categories of bordisms and reflection TQFTs

Pedro Brunialti · 2610.23712
title: (infinity,n)abstract: segal spacemath.ATmath-phmath.CTmath.MP
Abstract

We construct a strict involution, that is, a strict $\mathbb{Z}/2$-action, on the $(\infty,n)$-category $\mathbf{Bord}_n(H_n)$ of bordisms with $H_n$-structure, where $H_n$ is the symmetry type of a TQFT, using the complete $n$-fold Segal space model. Using this involution, we develop an appropriate definition of reflection TQFTs in this setting and show that they correspond to certain homotopy fixed point spaces. Finally, we apply the machinery to the example of super algebras, where, depending on the symmetry type, the homotopy fixed point data corresponds to either a stellar structure or a real structure on the algebra that the reflection theory assigns to the point.

title: equivariantabstract: g-spectrmath.AT
Abstract

We explore the analogy between rational G-spectra for an r-torus G and sheaves over an r-dimensional scheme, in the context of the author's algebraic model for rational G-spectra. In particular, the resolution of the sphere of a virtual representation in the standard model corresponds precisely to the Cousin complex resolution of a line bundle. The tom Dieck splitting for the sphere of an actual respresentation corresponds to the collapse of the Cousin spectral sequence by degrees, and the the non collapse in other cases corresponds to familiar geometric behaviour.

abstract: motivic filtrationabstract: motivic cohomologabstract: algebraic k-theormath.AGmath.ATmath.KT
Abstract

We fully classify vector bundles of critical rank on normal affine surfaces and threefolds over algebraically closed fields via their Chern classes. We also prove existence of rank $2$ bundles on threefolds with prescribed first and second motivic Chern classes. The main input is the motivic filtration on algebraic $K$-theory: its third piece is generated by smooth closed points and is identified with motivic cohomology by the third Chern class.

Homology and carrier faces of simplicial distributions

Aziz Kharoof, Chung-Ping Lai · 2610.23672
math.AT
Abstract

We study the facial structure of simplicial distribution polytopes using topological and algebraic methods. In the theory of simplicial distributions, measurement and event spaces are represented by simplicial sets, and compatible probability assignments form a convex polytope. Given a simplicial distribution, it is known that its supports determine its carrier face. Moreover, the supports form a simplicial subset of the event space. This naturally raises the question of how the topology of this simplicial subset is related to the corresponding carrier face. Our main objective is to relate the dimension of the carrier face to the topology and combinatorial structure of the space of supports. For $n$-facet-paired measurement spaces, we first express the carrier dimension in terms of the nullity of an associated facet matrix. When the measurement space is additionally orientable, we show that the carrier dimension is determined by the top-dimensional homology of the space of supports, yielding, in particular, a homological criterion for detecting vertices. We then develop an algebraic reduction based on a strong connectivity relation on the generating simplices of the space of supports. This construction yields a reduced facet matrix, indexed by the strongly connected components, whose null space is isomorphic to that of the original facet matrix. As a consequence, both the carrier dimension and extremality can be determined from the topology of the support and the way its strongly connected components are glued together.

mixed Hodge structures on the strictly upper triangular Lie algebras

Taito Shimoji · 2610.23505
math.AGmath.ATmath.DG
Abstract

We show that, for $k\geq 3$, a lattice in the unitriangular Lie group $U_k(\mathbb{R})$ is the fundamental group of a smooth quasi-projective complex algebraic variety if and only if $k=3$. The proof uses the conjugation symmetry and weight conditions of mixed Hodge structure on the Malcev Lie algebra proven by Morgan. This result identifies a family of groups that must be excluded in the search for smooth quasi-projective varieties with torsion-free nilpotent fundamental groups of high nilpotency class.

Operations on $G$-sets and the Dress map

Thomas Brazelton, Ben Spitz · 2610.23758
math.AT
Abstract

We study versions of binomial and multichoose coefficients for finite $G$-sets, extending a result of Siebeneicher from the 1970s concerning the duality between these operations. If the group $G$ comes from a finite Galois extension of fields, the Dress map provides a bridge from $G$-sets to quadratic forms over the base field. We show that this Dress map is compatible with binomial coefficients of finite $G$-sets and a particular operation on quadratic forms we define. This allows us to extend the definition of Brugall\'e and Wickelgren's quadratically enriched binomial coefficients to arbitrary Grothendieck--Witt classes. This allows for a new proof that quadratically enriched binomial coefficients depend only on the trace form of the inputted \'etale algebra, as well as an elegant reproof of a result of Chen and Wickelgren about binomial coefficients over finite fields.

Unitary $S^1$-Manifolds with Four Fixed Points and Kosniowski's Conjecture

Zhi L\"u, Jun Ma, Qiangbo Tan · 2610.23855
math.AT
Abstract

We study unitary \(S^1\)-manifolds with four isolated fixed points in relation to Kosniowski's conjectural inequality \(4\chi(M)>\dim M\). For every integer \(m\ge3\), we show that there exists a unitary \(S^1\)-manifold \(M_m^{18}\) with exactly four isolated fixed points, and these manifolds are pairwise distinct and nonzero in ordinary complex bordism. Since \(\chi(M_m^{18})=4\), they give an infinite family of counterexamples to \(4\chi(M)>\dim M\). By gluing two blocks formed by \(G_2/SU(3)\cong S^6\) and the unit disk $D(V)$ of 12- dimensional real $S^1$-representation $V$ along their equivariantly identified free boundaries, we explicitly construct such a manifold $M^{18}_3$. We also prove that the four-point case is null-bordant in real dimension \(16\) and in every real dimension greater than \(18\); hence \(18\) is the smallest dimension in which such a counterexample occurs.
