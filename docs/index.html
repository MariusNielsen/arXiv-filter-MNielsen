An $\infty$-category approach to $A_\infty$-algebras and $A_\infty$-categories

Dominic Joyce · 2611.24690
title: infinity-categorabstract: (infinity,1)abstract: quasicategormath.CTmath.SG
Abstract

$A_\infty$-algebras over a commutative ring $R$ are generalizations of $R$-algebras in which multiplication is only associative up to higher homotopies. Similarly, $A_\infty$-categories over $R$ are generalizations of $R$-linear categories in which composition of morphisms is only associative up to higher homotopies. They are important in the definition of Fukaya categories in Symplectic Geometry, and in other areas of mathematics. By $\infty$-categories we mean quasicategories, a class of simplicial sets which are a model for $(\infty,1)$-categories, used in the work of Jacob Lurie. We define and study $\infty$-categories of $A_\infty$-algebras and $A_\infty$-categories. Our approach is unusual in that rather than defining them by localization, we construct our $\infty$-categories completely explicitly as simplicial sets. Our $\infty$-categories are intended for applications to Fukaya categories in Symplectic Geometry. Given a suitable symplectic manifold $(X,\omega)$, one should construct its Fukaya category ${\mathscr F}(X,\omega)$, a (filtered) $A_\infty$-category, and its derived Fukaya category $D^b{\mathscr F}(X,\omega)$, a triangulated category (or better, a stable $\infty$-category). Typically ${\mathscr F}(X,\omega)$ depends on many arbitrary choices. Our $\infty$-categories give a clean way to explain in what sense ${\mathscr F}(X,\omega)$ and $D^b{\mathscr F}(X,\omega)$ are independent of these choices.

An Atiyah--Hirzebruch to Adams map and the hastened Adams spectral sequence for supercohomology

Arun Debray, Weicheng Ye, Matthew Yu · 2611.24725
title: adams spectral sequencetitle: spectral sequenceabstract: filtered spectrmath.ATcond-mat.str-elhep-thmath-ph
Abstract

We construct a map of filtered spectra inducing a map from the Atiyah--Hirzebruch spectral sequence (AHSS) to the Baker--Lazarev Adams spectral sequence of a $\mathit{ko}$-module $M$. Using this and a theorem of Bruner--Rognes, we construct a hastened Adams spectral sequence (HASS) for the Postnikov truncations of $\mathit{ko}$ and the connective Adams summand. Then, motivated by questions in quantum field theory, topological phases of matter, and tensor category theory, we apply the HASS to supercohomology, the cohomology theory $I_{\mathbb{C}^\times}(\tau_{\le 2}\mathit{ko})$, and observe that in examples of interest it is easier to calculate with than either the AHSS or the usual Adams spectral sequence. We include a User's Guide to the HASS and several worked examples, as well as hastened Adams charts for many commonly encountered $\mathcal{A}(1)$-modules.

Categorified Koszul duality and deformation theory of higher categories

Isamu Iwanari · 2611.23899
title: higher categortitle: koszul dualabstract: (infinity,n)abstract: presentablemath.AGmath.ATmath.CT
Abstract

This paper develops a deformation theory for stable $(\infty,n)$-categories for $n > 1$. We introduce the framework of complete $t$-structured stable presentable $(\infty,n)$-categories. Within this setting, we establish a higher categorified Koszul duality among complete $t$-structured presentable $(\infty,n)$-categories. Using this duality, we develop the deformation theory. We prove that the deformation problem for such categories is a well-behaved formal moduli problem determined by Hochschild cohomology, and we deduce the corresponding obstruction theory. Finally, we apply our results to sheaves of categories on perfect stacks.

Chromatic heights of homotopy fixed points of iterated topological Hochschild homology of connective spectra

Jiaxi Zha · 2611.23819
title: topological hochschild homologyabstract: chromatic redshiftabstract: ring spectrabstract: e_infinity-ringabstract: algebraic k-theormath.AT
Abstract

Let $R$ be an $\mathbb{E}_{\infty}$-ring spectrum of height $m$. The chromatic redshift theorem implies that its $n$-fold iterated algebraic $K$-theory $K^{(n)}(R)$ has height $m+n$. Moreover, there is a natural map $K^{(n)}(R)\to (T^n\otimes R)^{hT^n}$, where $T^n\otimes R$ denotes the $n$-fold iterated topological Hochschild homology. Hence it is natural to ask whether the target also has height $m+n$. Although this fails in general, we prove that it holds whenever $R$ is connective and $m\geq1$. Our proof is based on an iterative calculation using Wilson spaces and relative topological Hochschild homology. As an application, we determine the support of the $T^n$-homotopy fixed points of $n$-fold iterated topological Hochschild homology of $\mathrm{BP}\langle m\rangle$ for $n=2,3$ with $\mathrm{BP}\langle m\rangle$ equipped with any $\mathbb{E}_3$-$\mathrm{MU}$-form.

A simplicial model for manifold calculus through the configuration category

Rodrigue Haya Enriquez · 2611.24703
abstract: infinity-categormath.AT
Abstract

Let $M$ be a closed $d$-dimensional smooth manifold and $X:\Delta^{op}\rightarrow\operatorname{Fin}$ a finite simplicial set whose geometric realization is homeomorphic to $M$. Given the $\infty$-category $\mathcal{D}\mathrm{isk}_{/M}$ of disks embedded in $M$ and the path components functor $\pi_0:\mathcal{D}\mathrm{isk}_{/M}\rightarrow\operatorname{Fin}$, we construct a cofinal functor $\Gamma_X:\Delta^{op}\rightarrow\mathcal{D}\mathrm{isk}_{/M}$ such that $X=\pi_0\circ\Gamma_X$. This result provides an alternative construction of a simplicial model developed by Boavida de Brito, Lambrechts, Pryor and Songhafouo Tsopm\'en\'e to compute the space-valued presheaf $\operatorname{Emb}(-,M):\mathcal{D}\mathrm{isk}^{op}\rightarrow\mathcal{S}$. We also extend the result to manifolds with a $G$-structure and discuss applications to manifold calculus, factorization homology, and a possible generalization to manifolds with boundary.

Finite regularity moduli stacks through diffeology

Mahmud Azam, Steven Rayan · 2611.23451
abstract: presentablemath.DGmath.AGmath.CTmath.CVcross
Abstract

Diffeological non-Abelian Hodge theory motivates the study of $C^r$ families of Higgs bundles and flat bundles for finite $r$. In this article, we provide a uniform language for treating moduli stacks parametrizing such objects in terms of diffeology. More specifically, we develop a theory of stacks presentable by diffeological groupoids over the site of $C^r$ manifolds for arbitrary $r$. As an application, we construct moduli stacks over the sites of $C^r$ manifolds parametrizing $C^r$ vector bundles over a smooth manifold, and $C^r$ families of Higgs bundles and of flat bundles over a complex manifold. We also construct $C^r$ versions of the non-Abelian Hodge filtration stack, interpolating between Higgs bundles and flat bundles through $\lambda$--connections. We show that all these stacks are presentable by diffeological groupoids. Finally, we construct moduli stacks parametrizing $C^r$ families of $\Delta^n$--shaped diagrams of these objects, and show that they assemble into simplicial stacks whose levels are all presentable by diffeological groupoids.

Semi-exact functors and the conjecture of Coulembier and Flake

Alexander Sherman · 2611.24471
abstract: balmer spectrmath.RTmath.CT
Abstract

We introduce the notion of semi-exact functors between abelian categories, which are characterised by either preserving or breaking exactness of sequences. We prove semi-exact functors are always exact with respect to a canonically determined exact structure. For a symmetric tensor category $\Cs$, we define a support theory by considering all $\Bbbk$-linear symmetric monoidal, semi-exact functors $\mathscr{C}\to\operatorname{Ver}_{p^\infty}$ up to equivalence, and conjecture that it agrees with the Balmer spectrum of the stable category when $\mathscr{C}$ is finite. We prove a weak form of this conjecture and other results for Tannakian categories and in characteristic 0. Critical to the positive characteristic case is a functor constructed by Coulembier and Flake who were interested in higher Frobenius functors. We show that their construction, which relies on the theory of abelian envelopes, gives a natural source for semi-exact functors, and at least in the Tannakian setting gives all $\Bbbk$-linear, symmetric monoidal, semi-exact functors.

Abstract

Following Botvinnik--Rosenberg \cite{BotvinnikRosenberg2023}, we define fibered-boundary bordism, introduce sphere-bundle bordism, and prove an exact sequence relating them to ordinary bordism. The sequence also holds when all sphere fibers are required to be non-$D^r$-bounding. A stratified version of geometric realizations

math.AT
Abstract

Given a simplicial set $X$, let $\operatorname{B}({X})$ denote its poset of nondegenerate simplices. We define a natural stratification $\left\vert{X}\right\vert\rightarrow\operatorname{B}({X})$. math.SGmath.ATcross
Abstract

We show that, after attaching a subcritical handle to $T^*\bP^2$ to obtain a manifold $M$, the integral wrapped Floer endomorphism algebra of a cotangent fibre admits no spherical refinement for any choice of brane structure; this obstructs the existence of any wrapped Fukaya category for $M$ over the sphere spectrum. Cosmetic crossings and the knot complement problem

Seong-Jin Lee · 2611.24532
math.GTmath.ATcross
Abstract

We prove the cosmetic crossing conjecture, Problem 1.58 in Kirby's list, and the generalized cosmetic crossing conjecture for every knot whose branched double cover is an L-space; in particular, both hold for all alternating and all quasi-alternating knots. We also prove the oriented knot complement conjecture, Problem 1.81(D), for all closed 3-manifolds with finite fundamental group, in particular for all lens spaces: in such a manifold, every knot whose exterior is irreducible and not a solid torus is determined by its complement. Both results rest on a monotonicity theorem for Heegaard Floer correction terms: if a knot in a rational homology sphere has an isotropic, nonzero homology class, then the sum of all correction terms strictly increases along its surgeries with coefficients -1/j relative to the rational longitude, so no nontrivial surgery yields the original oriented manifold. We also show that every purely cosmetic band surgery on a knot with an L-space branched double cover is weakly nugatory, which answers a question of Rogers for these knots.

Dessins d'Enfants as a Topological Lens on Temporal Hypergraphs

Mehmet Emin Aktas, Esra Akbas · 2611.24613
cs.SImath.ATmath.COcross
Abstract

Hypergraphs are the natural language for group interactions, and topological data analysis has given us several ways to look at them: simplicial and cell complexes, persistent homology, and sheaves. All of these treat a hyperedge as an unordered set. In this paper, we introduce a different and, to our knowledge, new viewpoint that comes from Grothendieck's theory of dessins d'enfants and, unlike these approaches, takes into account the order in which vertices appear in each hyperedge and hyperedges appear around each vertex. A dessin is a bipartite graph drawn on a closed orientable surface. Through Walsh's classical correspondence between hypermaps and bipartite maps, every hypergraph equipped with a cyclic order of incidences at each vertex and inside each hyperedge is a dessin, and therefore determines a surface with faces and a genus. We develop this idea for temporal hypergraphs and build the surface by replaying history: incidences are added in the order in which they occurred, and each new incidence is inserted into the cyclic orders built so far, next to the earlier incidence to which it is related. We prove that every insertion either grows the hypergraph without creating a cycle, closes a cycle locally by splitting a face, or closes it globally by adding a handle; the number of independent cycles equals the number of local plus handle events, and the genus equals the number of handle events. These counts give interpretable, size-controlled measures of how a temporal hypergraph's cycles organize on a surface as it grows. We apply our method to five Stack Exchange communities and show that it separates communities that standard statistics cannot tell apart and quantifies how much the real order of interactions matters. We further discuss what the surface viewpoint adds to existing higher-order network analysis and how it opens a path toward dessin-based learning on hypergraphs.

From Persistent Cohomology to Maps into Arbitrary Eilenberg-MacLane Spaces

Tony Xiaochen Xiao, Jose A. Perea · 2611.23659
math.ATcs.CG
Abstract

In this paper, we address the construction of Eilenberg-MacLane coordinates (functions from datasets to Eilenberg-MacLane spaces $K(G,n)$) that encode nontrivial persistent cohomology classes in arbitrary dimensions $n$ and coefficient group $G$. The construction leverages the connecting homomorphism in the long exact sequence in \v{C}ech cohomology, from the short exact sequence of sheaves of continuous maps with values in $G$, $EG$ and $BG$. Crucially, the derivation is constructive: we give an explicit, recursive formula for preimages under the connecting homomorphism, which yields a constructive version of a classical cohomology vanishing theorem and, in turn, an algorithm for general Eilenberg-MacLane coordinates. We validate our approach through experiments on synthetic datasets, and present novel examples of $K(\mathbb{Z}/2, 2)$-coordinates not addressed in prior work.

Optimal homological stability: bounding secondary terms of Cohen-Lenstra moments and $S_d$-fields over $\mathbb{F}_q[t]$

Zhao Yu Ma · 2611.23721
math.NTmath.AGmath.ATmath.QAcross
Abstract

We prove an explicit stability line for the homology $H_i(\mathrm{Hur}_n^c,\mathbb{Q})$ of any Hurwitz space associated to a finite rack $c$ depending only on its homology up to some finite $n$. Moreover, as the range of $n$ increases, the slope of the resulting stability line converges to the optimal slope. Thus, for any Hurwitz space, a finite computation will yield near-optimal bounds on secondary terms for the corresponding arithmetic statistics problem. Our theorem also applies more generally to higher order stability, Frobenius weights, and braided vector spaces. As a special case of our theorem, we can deduce stability lines depending only on when $H_0(\mathrm{Hur}^c_n,\mathbb{Q})$ stabilizes, which are sharp in some cases. We apply this to two families of Hurwitz spaces to obtain bounds on the secondary term for (1) the number of $S_d$-degree $d$ function fields with squarefree discriminant, and (2) Cohen-Lenstra moments.
