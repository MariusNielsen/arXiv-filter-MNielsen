The Balmer spectrum of integral permutation modules

Umesh V Dubey, Juan Omar G\'omez · 2507.05892
author: Juan Omar Gómeztitle: balmer spectrmath.RTmath.AGmath.CTreplace
Abstract

We study the Balmer spectrum of the small derived category of permutation modules of a finite group over a commutative Noetherian ring. In this generality, we obtain a set-theoretic description of the spectrum. Under certain mild additional assumptions on the base ring, we describe its topology by reducing the problem first to the modular locus and then to the case of elementary abelian groups. Furthermore, we introduce a twisted cohomology ring in this context, which allows us to realize the spectrum, in the case of elementary abelian $p$-groups, as a Dirac scheme. This extends recent work of Balmer and Gallauer from the setting of ground fields to the more general setting of commutative Noetherian rings.

Abstract

We construct a solidification functor $\LH$ on condensed anima by localizing at the maps inverted by solid integral chains. A pointed connected nilpotent condensed anima is $\Hsol$-local precisely when its fundamental group admits a finite central series with solid abelian factors and its higher homotopy groups are solid. $\mathbb{Q}_p$-analytic intersections of $\mathbb{C}_p$-analytic manifolds with perfectoid Banach--Colmez spaces

Sean Howe, Ray Li · 2611.31104
abstract: lubin-tatemath.AGmath.NT
Abstract

We show that transverse intersections between smooth $\mathbb{C}_p$-analytic manifolds in $\mathbb{C}_p^n$ and perfectoid Banach--Colmez spaces of complementary $\mathbb{C}_p$-dimension are $n$-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}_p$-analytic manifolds. In the case of the Banach--Colmez space $\mathrm{BC}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)$, this is a strong form of the relativistic $p$-adic sunscreen conjecture. These results hold more generally with $\mathbb{C}_p$ replaced by an arbitrary complete algebraically closed extension of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, and we also establish some non-linear analogs for infinite level Lubin--Tate spaces.

abstract: presentablemath.KT
Abstract

We construct a presentable, stable \(\infty\)-category \(\bKK\), and exhibit it as the target of the universal functor from the category of bornological algebras into cocomplete, stable \(\infty\)-categories, preserving filtered colimits, and satisfying smooth homotopy invariance, excision, and stabilisation with respect to Schatten-class or smooth compact operators. Abstract

In 1977 Katz asked for an explicit lower bound on the $p$-adic valuation of $(D^nf)(0)$, where $D$ is the normalized invariant derivation of a one-parameter formal group of finite height $h$ over a ring of $p$-adic integers and $f$ is a power series with integral coefficients. He answered the question at height two, for functions whose translates by the points of order $p$ sum to zero, with the bound $\lfloor np/(p^2-1)\rfloor$. For Lubin--Tate groups over unramified bases, results of Bannai and Kobayashi give the bound $c_hn-(h-1)$, where $c_h=\frac1{p-1}-\frac1{p^h-1}$. We prove this bound for every one-dimensional formal group whose multiplication by $p$ is divisible by $p$ below degree $p^h$, over any $p$-torsion-free $\mathbb Z_{(p)}$-algebra and for every integral $f$, together with the bound $\sum_{h\nmid j}\lfloor n/p^j\rfloor$. The slope $c_h$, the constant $h-1$, and the second bound at the powers of $p$ cannot be improved. At height two it follows that Katz's bound holds for every integral function except at the orders divisible by $p^2-1$, where it can fail by one, and it does fail at the first of them. Over a ramified base the hypothesis cannot be weakened to the height of the special fibre, while an action of the Teichmüller roots of unity restores it. For the Cartier dual of the Honda formal group of height $h$ we construct an integral model whose $h-1$ coordinate directions have the sharp slopes $p^i/(p^h-1)$, which add up to $c_h$, each with the optimal constant $1$. The height-two case is the local exponent in the second step of the timeline in Alpöge's note on the Riemann hypothesis, where the bound is stated with a logarithmic loss; for integral functions on the formal group the loss is the constant $1$. A compact nonformal manifold with holonomy Spin(7)

Yaroslav Bazaikin · 2611.29751
math.DGmath.ATcross
Abstract

We construct a compact simply connected eight-manifold with holonomy Spin(7) and a triple Massey product of degrees $(3,2,2)$ that does not contain zero. The manifold is a resolution of a flat orbifold. Joyce's gluing theorem gives it a torsion-free Spin(7) structure. The resolution map factors through a quotient of $Y\times S^1$, where $Y$ is Mart\'in-Merch\'an's nonformal $G_2$ manifold. The map to this quotient has degree one, so pullback preserves a nonzero Massey pairing. On the resolution the detecting degree-two class annihilates the full indeterminacy. Simple connectivity and nonformality imply full Spin(7) holonomy. The manifold has $(b_2,b_3,b_4^+,b_4^-)=(22,10,91,27)$.

A fourfold Toda bracket that is not defined, although both threefold subbrackets contain zero

Osamu Nishimura · 2611.29239
math.AT
Abstract

We give an explicit example, in the stable homotopy groups of spheres, of a fourfold Toda bracket that is not defined although both of its threefold subbrackets contain zero. Namely, $0\in\langle\nu^2,2,\eta^2\rangle$ and $0\in\langle 2,\eta^2,\nu\rangle$, but $\langle\nu^2,2,\eta^2,\nu\rangle$ has no defining system in the sense of Kochman. This answers a question of Isaksen. The computational input consists only of relations from Toda's book, together with an explicit choice of null-homotopies. The same holds, in the sense of Walker, for the unstable quadruple $(\nu_n^2,2\iota_{n+6},\eta_{n+6}^2,\nu_{n+8})$ for every $n\ge10$. Moreover, for $n\ge10$, even Cohen's fourfold bracket of this quadruple is empty.

math.ATmath.CT
Abstract

We construct the Grandis model structure on directed spaces with $\Delta$-generated underlying spaces and on the full subcategory of $\Delta$-Hausdorff objects. In either setting the model structure is left proper, and its weak equivalences are precisely the maps that induce equivalences of fundamental categories in the sense of Grandis. Moore spaces, polyhedral products, and positive scalar curvature

Satyanath Howladar, Lorenzo Ruffoni · 2611.29073
math.GTmath.ATmath.GRcross
Abstract

Gromov conjectured that the macroscopic dimension of the universal cover of a closed $n$-manifold of positive scalar curvature is at most $n-2$. Gromov's conjecture holds for a closed spin manifold of dimension $n\geq 5$, provided that its fundamental group has property $(*)_m$, i.e., its classifying space is stably homotopy equivalent to a wedge of Moore spaces. We prove property $(*)_m$ for a large class of groups, including for instance the fundamental groups of closed hyperbolic $3$-manifolds, the commutator subgroups of right-angled Coxeter groups with low-dimensional nerves, pure braid groups, and many graph products thereof. As an additional application of independent interest, we show that there are finite-index subgroups of right-angled Artin groups that do not have property $(*)_m$ and can have arbitrary torsion in their (co)homology in any degree.

The M\"obius Homology Laplacian for Persistence Modules

Tatum Rask · 2611.28292
math.ATmath.CO
Abstract

Persistent homology provides a framework for studying the evolution of topological features across filtered spaces. While persistence diagrams give discrete invariants that record the birth and death of homological features, they do not directly encode analytic or geometric structure associated to persistence modules. This paper develops a spectral framework for persistent homology by introducing and studying a Laplace operator on the M\"obius chain complex, called the M\"obius homology Laplacian. M\"obius homology categorifies M\"obius inversion for persistence modules by replacing integer-valued data with vector-space-valued chain complexes. By equipping these chain complexes with inner products, we define a Laplace operator on the M\"obius chain complex whose kernel provides canonical cycle representatives for the associated M\"obius homology space. We analyze the spectral properties of this operator in two primary settings: one-parameter persistence modules and birth-death modules arising from filtrations of simplicial complexes. For one-parameter modules, we completely characterize the eigenvalues of the degree-one M\"obius homology Laplacian. For birth-death modules, we show that the trace and determinant of the degree-zero M\"obius homology Laplacian reflect the combinatorial structure of the persistence diagram.

The Seifert--van Kampen Theorem

Djamal Ait-Akli · 2611.28785
math.AT
Abstract

The Seifert--van Kampen theorem computes the fundamental group of a space from an open cover by two path-connected subspaces, provided their intersection is path-connected. This article gives a self-contained account of the theorem, explains its universal-property formulation as a pushout in the category of groups, and derives several standard computations. Upper bound $2^{n}-1$ for chromatic number of orbit polytope of a small cover is attained for every $n\geq4$

Djordje Baralic, Riste Skrekovski · 2611.31219
math.ATmath.CO
Abstract

Davis and Januszkiewicz proved that the facets of a simple $n$-polytope $P$ which is the orbit space of a small cover can be properly coloured by $2^{n}-1$ colours. The bound is sharp for $n=2$, it is not sharp for $n=3$ by the Four Color Theorem, and it was shown to be sharp for $n=4$ by the first author. We show that it is sharp for every $n\geq4$. The polytope is obtained from the dual of the cyclic polytope $C^{n}(2^{n}-1)$ by truncations of faces of codimension at least $3$. The resolution works over an arbitrary field and gives the sharp bound $(q^{n}-1)/(q-1)$ over the field with $q$ elements. More generally, every simple $n$-polytope whose facets can be properly coloured by $k\leq2^{n}-1$ colours becomes, after truncations of faces of codimension at least $3$, the orbit polytope of a small cover with the same chromatic number $k$. We also construct, for every $n\geq4$, a simple $n$-polytope with $\chi=2^{n}-1$ which is the orbit space of both a small cover and a quasitoric manifold, and in the oriented case we obtain the sharp bound $2^{n-1}$ for every $n\geq4$.
