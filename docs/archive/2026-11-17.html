Revisiting the $\beta_1$-action on the 3-primary stable homotopy groups of spheres

Jack Morgan Davies · 2511.02324
author: Jack Morgan Daviestitle: stable homotopyabstract: synthetic spectrabstract: adams-novikovabstract: adams operationmath.ATmath.KTreplace
Abstract

Let $\beta_1$ be the first 3-torsion class in the stable homotopy groups of spheres in even degree. Toda showed that $\beta_1^5 \neq 0$, whilst $\beta_1^6 = 0$. Shimomura generalised this to the 144-periodic family generated by $\beta_1$, written as $\{\beta_{1+9s}\}_{s\geq 0}$, and showed that any $5$-fold product $\prod_5 \beta_{1+9s} \neq 0$, whilst all 6-fold products $\prod_6 \beta_{1+9s} = 0$. In this article, we give a simple proof of these results as well as some generalisations to other 144-periodic families. Along the way, we also refine known detection results for these families. Our tools include BP-synthetic spectra, and the well-known Adams--Novikov spectral sequence for the spectrum of topological modular forms at the prime $3$ as well as its Adams operations.

An $(\infty,1)$-category of conically smooth stratified bordisms

Diogo Freire de Andrade · 2611.19459
title: (infinity,1)abstract: segal spacemath.ATmath.CT
Abstract

To study derived defect topological quantum field theories, we construct an $(\infty,1)$-category of stratified bordisms and sketch its $(\infty,d)$-dimensional analogue. In the first chapters we develop a method for constructing symmetric monoidal $(\infty,1)$-categories, inspired by Segal spaces but better suited to geometric inputs, which we call interval Segal spaces. Using this framework, we construct the $(\infty,1)$-category of $d$-dimensional smooth manifolds via smooth $\infty$-sheaves thereof, in an attempt to address fibrancy issues present in prior work on this subject. We further equip this category with a symmetric monoidal structure, compute its homotopy 1-category, and extend the construction to manifolds endowed with tangential structures, obtaining the interval Segal space of $d$-dimensional bordisms with tangential structure. To model the stratifications supporting topological defects, we work with conically smooth stratified spaces, a suitably smooth framework for conical stratifications. We define stratified topological manifolds as stratified $\mathcal{B}$-manifolds for a suitable $(\infty,1)$-category of basics $\mathcal{B}$. Using these, we construct an interval Segal space whose objects are compact closed stratified topological $(d-1)$-manifolds and whose 1-morphisms are stratified $d$-dimensional bordisms, and we endow it with a symmetric monoidal structure. We also use tangential structures on conically smooth stratified spaces to formulate a notion of defect labels, aiming toward bordisms with labeled defects in the style of Carqueville et al. Finally, we sketch a higher-dimensional generalization of the interval category to a category of $d$-cubes for $d>1$, conjecturally presenting $(\infty,d)$-categories, and outline how stratified factorization homology may be used to produce derived stratified defect TQFTs.

Cohesive 2-Topoi: Foundational Aspects and Nonabelian Differential Cohomology

Yuri Ximenes Lecca, Willian Goulart Gomes Velasco · 2611.00532
title: -topoimath-phHexagon
Abstract

We provide a self-contained exposition of cohesive $(2,1)$-topos theory, its internal synthetic geometry, and its realization of intrinsic, modal, and differential nonabelian cohomology. Starting from the $(2,1)$-categorical hierarchy, Grothendieck $(2,1)$-topoi, effective internal groupoids, and $\E_k$-algebra objects over $(2,1)$-operads, we develop a detailed comparison between \emph{internal} and \emph{external} deloopings in $(2,1)$-presheaf and Grothendieck $(2,1)$-topoi. Specifically, we formalize external delooping by transporting the canonical delooping transformation along the group, discrete, and groupoid Yoneda 2-embeddings, proving that it is 2-naturally equivalent to the internal delooping in any Grothendieck $(2,1)$-topos. We then establish the 2-truncated Dunn--Lurie additivity, Baez--Dolan stabilization, and Eckmann--Hilton characterization of $k$-tuply groupal objects as groupal $\E_k$-algebras stabilizing at $k=3$, and clarify the truncation behavior of iterated deloopings $B_{\mathbf{H}}^k G$. Finally, we review Lawvere--Schreiber cohesive modalities, construct abstract principal $k$-bundles, the flat de Rham modality $\flat_{\dR}$, universal Maurer--Cartan forms $\theta_k$, connection moduli stacks $B^k_{\con}G$, and sharp-adjusted internal connection moduli $G\Conn^k(X)$. The paper culminates in synthetic differential cohomology, where we discuss extensions of the Maurer--Cartan morphism to non-groupal coefficients via $\int_{\dR} \dashv \flat_{\dR}$, and internal classifying structures.

Equivariant intersection cohomology of some $4$-dimensional locally standard $T$-pseudomanifolds

Yuya Koike, Shintaro Kuroki · 2611.19010
title: equivariantmath.ATmath.GT
Abstract

Locally standard $T$-pseudomanifolds are singular analogues of quasitoric manifolds. In this paper, we study a certain class of four-dimensional locally standard $T^2$-pseudomanifolds over convex polygons. We establish an Orlik-Raymond type classification and study their ordinary and equivariant cohomology, as well as their intersection and equivariant intersection cohomology. We also introduce ring structures on middle-perversity intersection cohomology and equivariant intersection cohomology via change of perversity. We show that these rings can be described in terms of the cohomology and equivariant cohomology of associated nonsingular spaces, which are quasitoric manifolds or $S^4$. Furthermore, we give combinatorial descriptions of the equivariant cohomology and equivariant intersection cohomology in terms of labelled graphs.

Motivic Borel-Moore Homology is Borel-Moore Motivic Homology

Spencer G. Taylor · 2611.19346
title: motivicabstract: presentableabstract: motivic homotopymath.AGmath.KT
Abstract

This paper studies the higher Chow groups of arbitrary finite type separated schemes using motivic homotopy theory, serving as a Borel-Moore counterpart to the $\mathbb{A}^1$-invariant motivic cohomology of Bachmann-Elmanto-Morrow. We construct a filtration on the G-theory spectrum of a finite type separated scheme $X$, inducing an Atiyah-Hirzebruch style spectral sequence whose associated graded pieces are a complex computing the higher Chow groups of $X$. We prove that this complex is representable in the stable motivic homotopy category, and is a module over the $\mathbb{A}^1$-motivic complex, giving rise to an action of $\mathbb{A}^1$-invariant motivic cohomology on the Bloch-Levine cycle complex. This generalizes some results of Bloch and Levine relating higher Chow groups and motivic cohomology to the case of schemes over an arbitrary base, and which need not be smooth or equidimensional.

The $J$-Homomorphism From Localising The Cobordism Hypothesis

Amartya Shekhar Dubey, Luuk Stehouwer · 2611.19615
title: j-homomorphismabstract: infinity-groupoidabstract: (infinity,n)math.ATmath-phmath.CTmath.MP
Abstract

Lurie's cobordism hypothesis classifies fully extended framed topological field theories valued in a symmetric monoidal $(\infty,n)$-category $\mathcal{C}$ with duals by the $\infty$-groupoid $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{fd}}$ of fully dualisable objects. This classification results in an $O(n)$-action on $\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{fd}}$, where $O(n)$ acts on the framed bordism category by change of framing. Lurie observed that for a connective spectrum, this action is ``a version of the classical $J$-homomorphism.'' We make this identification explicit: with respect to the right action of $O(n)$ on framings, the $O(n)$-action on the localisation $L(\mathrm{Bord}_n^{\mathrm{fr}}) \simeq S$ is precisely $\ominus J_n$, the inverse of the unstable $J$-homomorphism $J_n \colon O(n) \to \mathrm{GL}_1(S)$. The identification of the action with the unstable $J$-homomorphism in the setting of smooth stacks is due to Grady--Pavlov; we give an independent proof in Lurie's original formulation of the cobordism hypothesis, using the abstract Pontryagin--Thom construction. The sign (i.e.\ whether the action is $J_n$ or $\ominus J_n$) depends on the convention for the $O(n)$-action on framings, and is relevant for $\mathrm{pin}^\pm$ theories and hence for quantum systems with time-reversal symmetry. We compute the induced action on homotopy groups of the sphere spectrum in low degrees, yielding explicit invariants for invertible TFTs in dimensions $1$, $2$, and $3$, and connect these to the Kitaev chain, the framing anomaly, the spin-statistics theorem, and anomaly inflow. We recover the Galatius--Madsen--Tillmann--Weiss theorem as a consequence of the cobordism hypothesis and relate the homotopy fixed-point and mapping-spectrum perspectives on invertible TFTs via the duality between tangential and normal framings.

A Note on the Cosupport of Balmer-Favi Idempotents

Liangyu Zhu · 2611.19221
abstract: e(n)-localabstract: stable module categorabstract: tensor triangulatmath.CTmath.ACmath.AT
Abstract

Let $g_x$ be the Balmer-Favi idempotent at a point $x$ of the spectrum of a rigidly-compactly generated tensor triangulated category. We study the equality $\operatorname{Cosupp}(g_x)=\operatorname{gen}(x)$ by expressing $g_x$ as a local dual. We prove the equality for the derived category of a Noetherian scheme and for the stable module category of a finite group scheme over a field of positive characteristic. Capacities from the Chiu-Tamarkin complex II: Product formula and toric domains

Wenyuan Li, Bingyu Zhang · 2611.21220
abstract: ring spectrmath.SGmath.ATmath.KTcross
Abstract

In this article, we continue our study of equivariant symplectic capacities of open sets in a cotangent bundle defined via the Chiu-Tamarkin complex constructed from the microlocal theory of sheaves. The paper consists of two parts: 1) We develop the theory of $S^1$-equivariant Chiu-Tamarkin complex over complex-oriented ring spectra. 2) Applications of the theory to symplectic capacities: We show that a product formula for the capacities, whose Ekeland-Hofer capacities version is folklore since 1988, is not true via an (uncountable family of) counterexample(s). M\"obius Invertibility of Rank Invariants

Nathaniel Clause, Woojin Kim, Facundo M\'{e}moli · 2611.20463
abstract: presentablemath.AT
Abstract

One of the best known invariants of multi-parameter persistent homology is the rank invariant, which is subsumed by the generalized rank invariant (GRI), obtained by naturally extending its domain. M\"obius inversion of the GRI not only provides a compact encoding of this invariant but is also useful for clarifying its relationship with other invariants of multi-parameter persistent homology. However, M\"obius inversion of the GRI has been defined only under suitable finiteness conditions on its domain and support. This work studies the extent to which M\"obius inversion can be extended beyond these finiteness conditions. Namely, we formalize the notion of M\"obius invertibility, which captures when M\"obius inversion exists beyond these finiteness conditions, and investigate when the GRI is M\"obius invertible. A central result we establish is that M\"obius invertibility of the GRI is guaranteed for finitely presentable $d$-parameter persistence modules when $d\leq 2$, but may fail when $d>2$. Establishing this failure also corrects an error in the existing literature. In response to this higher-dimensional obstruction, we show that for finitely presentable $d$-parameter persistence modules, restricting the GRI to intervals subordinate to the finite grid supporting a minimal free presentation preserves its M\"obius inversion whenever the latter exists over all intervals. Since computing the GRI over all intervals in $\mathbb{R}^d$ is infeasible in practice, this result provides a principled justification for restricting the GRI to those intervals.

Quasi-F-split heights and derived Morita invariance

Keiho Matsumoto · 2611.20424
abstract: topological restriction homologyabstract: polygonicmath.AG
Abstract

In this paper, we study quasi-F-splitting from the viewpoint of non-commutative algebraic geometry. Let k be a perfect field of characteristic p. For a proper dg-category over k with a Serre functor, we define quasi-F^e-split heights for e\geq 1 by using finite topological restriction homology with coefficients in a shift of the Serre functor. Using the functoriality of polygonic topological Hochschild homology for laced categories due to Harpaz--Nikolaus--Saunier, we show that these heights are invariant under derived Morita equivalence. We study faithful Galois extensions in a presentably symmetric monoidal $\infty$-category $\mathscr{C}$ by a dualizable $\mathbb{E}_1$-group $G$. We study the Dedekind-MacNeille completion through representable functors on the wide subcategory $\mathbf{Pos}_{\mathrm{cl}}$ of posets and lower cut-continuous (closed) maps. A notion of pseudo-morphisms with applications to the study of polynomial maps and of link-homotopy groups of 2-string link

Benjamin Audoux · 2611.20304
math.GTmath.ATmath.GRcross
Abstract

We introduce a notion of pseudo-morphism for groups, which extends the notion of group morphism from a group to a ring. We show that pseudo-morphisms from a group $G$ to an integral domain $R$ form a filtred Hopf algebra which can be seen as an efficient notion of dual for $G$. We also show that pseudo-morphisms are closely related to the notion of polynomial maps in the sense of Eilenberg-MacLane/Passi, the two notions even coinciding when $G$ is finitely generated and $R$ is a field. It follows that, in that case, polynomial maps are endowed with an Hopf structure. In a second part, we study non repeated Milnor invariants (in the $4$-dimensional context of $2$-string links) from this pseudo-morphism point of view. We show that they form a sub-bialgebra of the Hopf algebra of pseudo-morphisms on the group of $2$-string links up to link-homotopy, and prove that it is isomorphic to an explicit quotient $\textrm{Mil}$ of a polynomial ring. This leads to a fully algebraic description of the group of 2-string links up to link-homotopy as the dual group associated to $\textrm{Mil}$. As a by-product, we obtain that non repeated Milnor invariants are polynomial maps, and we show that this can be used to give a new proof of their finite type property.

Abstract

The Extended Persistent Homology Transform (XPHT) describes a shape through the extended persistence diagrams of its directional height functions. We present an algorithm for computing a finite directional sample of the three-dimensional XPHT from a triangulated boundary. Our main construction determines essential first-homology pairings by combining an ordered tree-cotree decomposition with evaluation-dual cocycles. Cyclic Hasse diagrams of manifolds and noncommutative path cohomology

Sergei O. Ivanov, Hequn Zhang · 2611.19813
math.ATmath.CO
Abstract

Let $M$ be a connected oriented closed manifold of dimension $d\geq 2$, equipped with a regular CW structure, and let $P$ be its face poset. We consider the cyclic Hasse diagram $G_P^{\mathrm{cyc}}$, obtained from the directed Hasse diagram $G_P$ by adding an arrow from each maximal cell to every $0$-cell of $M$. Over an arbitrary field $\mathbb K$, we completely describe the path cohomology algebra $PH^*(G_P^{\mathrm{cyc}})$ in terms of the $\mathbb K$-Betti numbers of $M$. In particular, this algebra is not graded commutative whenever the dimension of $H^*(M)$ over $\mathbb K$ is at least 4. This disproves Grigor'yan's conjecture on graded commutativity. Moreover, as $M$ ranges over connected oriented closed surfaces, the even subalgebras $PH^{\mathrm{even}}(G_P^{\mathrm{cyc}})$, regarded as ungraded associative unital $\mathbb K$-algebras, satisfy no common algebraic identity beyond those satisfied by all associative unital $\mathbb K$-algebras. These results distinguish GLMY path cohomology from classical cohomology, whose cup product is graded commutative and whose even subalgebra is commutative. As an application, we construct a digraph $G$ for which the K\"unneth formula for the categorical product $G\times G$ fails, answering a question of Pinto negatively and disproving another conjecture of Grigor'yan.

Finite generation of the second homology group of the genus three Torelli group

Alexander A. Gaifullin · 2611.20353
math.GTmath.ATmath.GRcross
Abstract

We prove that the group $H_2(\mathcal{I}_3;\mathbb{Z})$ is finitely generated, where $\mathcal{I}_3$ is the Torelli subgroup of the mapping class group of a closed genus-three surface $\Sigma_3$. A key role in the proof is played by establishing the following property: for each symplectic transvection $t_x\in\mathrm{Sp}_{6}(\mathbb{Z})$, we have $$ \bigl(t^{120}_x-1\bigr)^{57}\bigl(t_x^{24}-1\bigr)(t_x-1)^6H_2(\mathcal{I}_3;\mathbb{Z})=0. $$ The proof is based on the study of the spectral sequences associated with the action of $\mathcal{I}_3$ on the Bestvina-Bux-Margalit complex of minimizing cycles and with the actions on certain auxiliary complexes of the stabilizers of two- and three-component multicurves on $\Sigma_3$. Homotopy Classification of Pointed Self-Maps of $\mathbb{RP}^3\#\mathbb{RP}^3$

Xinyu Liu, Xuezhi Zhao · 2611.19310
math.AT
Abstract

We classify pointed self-maps of $M=\mathbb{RP}^{3}\#\mathbb{RP}^{3}$ up to pointed homotopy. Using the universal cover $S^2\times\mathbb R$, we reduce the problem to paths in the sphere mapping space with even or odd endpoint conditions. The evaluation fibration, Whitehead products, and a double-coset calculation determine the resulting secondary invariants. The classes inducing the trivial fundamental-group endomorphism split into four families, each in bijection with $\mathbb Z$ after choosing a reference homotopy class. Every other realizable endomorphism sends both standard generators to reflections. For each such endomorphism, the realizable multipliers on $\pi_2(M)\cong\mathbb{Z}$ are precisely the odd integers $d$, and the classes with fixed multiplier are in bijection with $\mathbb Z/|d|\mathbb Z$ after choosing a reference homotopy class. We compute an explicit composition law on the double-reflection submonoid and determine the pointed self-homotopy equivalences. The classes inducing the trivial fundamental-group endomorphism form a subset $\mathcal{T}$ that is preserved by composition on either side with arbitrary pointed homotopy classes and satisfies $\mathcal{T}^2=\{0\}$, where $0$ is the constant class. For each fixed triple $(m,k,d)$ in the double-reflection case, the $|d|$ classes induce identical homomorphisms on every homotopy group. Thus the secondary invariant detects distinctions invisible to these induced homomorphisms.
