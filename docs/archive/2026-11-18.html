Enriched nerves, interleaving distances and the multiplicative magnitude-path spectral sequence

Katsuhiko Kuribayashi · 2611.22433
title: spectral sequencemath.AT
Abstract

By regarding the nerves of categories enriched over a monoidal poset as persistence simplicial sets, we investigate an upper bound of the interleaving distance in the homotopy category between the nerves in the sense of Lanary and Scoccola. In particular, it holds that the distance between the nerves for $r$-homotopy equivalent generalized metric spaces is less than or equal to the half of $r$. Moreover, a product on the cochain complex associated with a persistence simplicial set is introduced and related to the cup product on the magnitude cochain complex in the sense of Hepworth. As a consequence, the product gives the multiplicative structure to the cohomological magnitude-path spectral sequence (MPSS) whose first term is isomorphic to the magnitude cohomology ring. Furthermore, we discuss at which term the MPSS for a connected finite digraph collapses by using the interleaving of the enriched nerve of the digraph.

A bordism perspective on ends of manifolds

Fadi Mezher · 2611.23166
abstract: infinity-categorabstract: presentablemath.AT
Abstract

We refine the classical pro-homotopy type of the end of a manifold by recording the boundaries of a compact exhaustion together with the bordisms between them. We show that this assignment, the bordism-end, is functorial in embeddings, and independent of the choice of exhaustion. Its target is the free sequential colimit completion of the bordism $\infty$-category. Along the way of defining the bordism-end, we show that the category of all smooth $d$-dimensional manifolds and embeddings is formally obtained from the analogous category of compact manifolds. Through the formalism of the bordism-end, it follows that a manifold is diffeomorphic to the interior of a compact manifold if and only if its bordism-end is representable. Moreover, this formalism yields a fibre sequence relating the classifying spaces of the compactly supported diffeomorphism group of a manifold and its full diffeomorphism group, and a space of bordism-ends. Finally, we set up the pro-end functorially in embeddings, with target the cospan $\infty$-category of pro-spaces, and show that it factors through the bordism-end.

Calabi--Yau dimensions of monotone Fukaya summands from infinitesimal gradings

Yonghwan Kim · 2611.23163
abstract: infinity-categorabstract: straighteningmath.SGmath.AGcross
Abstract

We develop a theory of infinitesimal gradings for $\mathbb{Z}/2$-graded $A_\infty$-categories and use it to detect Calabi--Yau dimensions of monotone Fukaya summands from quantum cohomology. The dimensions we find agree with predictions from Serre functors of Kuznetsov components, such as $2$ for Gushel--Mukai and Verra fourfolds and the fractional value $\frac{n-2}{n}$ for the symplectic Grassmannian $\operatorname{IG}(2,2n)$. An infinitesimal grading induces endomorphisms on cohomological morphism spaces, and we prove a straightening theorem that realizes its weights as an actual grading compatible with the $A_\infty$-structure. We use this grading to show that Dehn twists give faithful braid group representations in the symplectic mapping class groups of Gushel--Mukai and Verra fourfolds.

The Nygaard graded piece, the Frobenius--Witt cotangent complex and regularity criteria

Ryo Ishizuka, Kanau Shimada · 2611.22214
abstract: prismaticabstract: syntomicmath.AGmath.ACmath.NT
Abstract

For a $p$-torsionfree $p$-quasisyntomic ring, we construct a fiber sequence relating the $p$-th Nygaard graded piece of absolute prismatic cohomology to the Frobenius--Witt cotangent complex. The latter is an arithmetic analogue of the cotangent complex in which the differential of $p$ need not vanish. We also clarify the relationship between the regularity criterion proved by Mao using the Frobenius--Witt cotangent complex and the regularity criterion established by Bhatt and Mathew using the Nygaard graded piece. This relationship was anticipated by Bhatt and Mathew.

The profinite shape equivalence

Tim Holzschuh · 2611.23061
abstract: infinity-topoabstract: straighteningabstract: sheaves of animamath.AGmath.AT
Abstract

We show that, for every $\infty$-topos $\mathcal{X}$, the profinite shape induces a canonical equivalence between the $\infty$-category of pro-objects in locally constant constructible sheaves of anima in $\mathcal{X}$ and the slice $\infty$-category of profinite anima over the profinite shape $\widehat{\Pi}_{\infty}(\mathcal{X})$. We deduce this profinite shape equivalence from the monodromy classification theorem by establishing a continuous version of the straightening-unstraightening equivalence for profinite anima. In addition, we explain how the monodromy classification theorem recovers its classical counterparts concerning locally constant sheaves of finite sets and of (abelian) groups.

Associative Log Rings and their Bimodules

Deik van der Horst, Tommy Lundemo · 2611.23064
math.ATmath.AGmath.KT
Abstract

We propose a definition of associative log rings. The role of the commutative monoid is replaced by a variant of a crossed module whose source is a monoid, and we use the resulting group action to control the failure of commutativity. This extends the classical notion of a log ring, even in the commutative case. Natural examples include algebras of logarithmic differential operators. The definition lends itself to a canonical construction of a log enveloping algebra and hence of log bimodules, and we prove that the resulting second log Hochschild cohomology group classifies log square-zero extensions. Along the way, we complete the logarithmic analog of Quillen's classification of abelian group objects in augmented algebras: In exact augmented associative log rings, abelian group objects recover log bimodules. Our constructions enjoy animated analogs, and we develop the basic theory of associative animated log rings \emph{en route} to our classification of exact log square-zero extensions.

Complete characterization of graphs with common maximal abelian cover

Frederico Can\c{c}ado, Gabriel Coutinho, Thom\'as Jung Spier · 2611.22993
math.COmath.ATcross
Abstract

The maximal abelian cover of a connected graph is the quotient of its universal cover by the commutator subgroup of its fundamental group. We provide a complete classification of finite connected graphs with isomorphic maximal abelian covers. For trees or for connected graphs with at least two cycles, the cover determines the base up to isomorphism; in cycle rank one, it determines the primitive cyclic pattern of rooted trees attached to the unique cycle. We also characterize when every automorphism descends to the base and give an orbit-size criterion for splitting the canonical lift extension, answering two questions raised by Baez in 2026.

Distance algebras of finite distributive lattices are Gorenstein

Aaron Chan, Bohan Xing · 2611.22513
math.ACmath.ATcross
Abstract

Distance algebras were introduced by Asao and Ivanov to realize magnitude homology and cohomology of quasi-metric spaces as Tor- and Ext-groups respectively. Let $\Sigma_L$ be the distance algebra of the Hasse graph of a finite distributive lattice $L$, where the metric is given by the lengths of shortest paths in the graph. We show that $\Sigma_L$ is Gorenstein over any Gorenstein commutative noetherian ring $R$, and give bound of its self-injective dimension. The proof constructs, for each indecomposable (relative) injective module, a projective resolution that is in general non-minimal. When $R=\Bbbk$ is a field, we use algebraic Morse theory to reduce it to a minimal resolution, so that the Betti numbers can be obtained as the dimensions of homologies of certain order complexes. As a consequence, we show that the self-injective dimension of $\Sigma_L$ is the maximal Castelnuovo--Mumford regularity of certain Stanley--Reisner rings. We also show that the first two terms of these minimal resolutions are multiplicity-free, whereas arbitrarily large multiplicities occur in every higher degree. In the appendix, we include a more abstract formulation of the non-minimal projective resolution, namely, a simplicial replacement of a diagram of projective modules.

Higher orbifold Euler characteristics of torus representations

Nathaniel Peters, Christopher Seaton · 2611.22555
math.RTmath.AT
Abstract

We compute the $\Gamma$-Euler characteristic of a finite-dimensional representation of the compact torus $\mathbb{T}^k$ where $\Gamma$ is an arbitrary finitely presented group. We additionally compute the $\Gamma$-Euler characteristic of the unit sphere in such a representation as well as the corresponding symplectic quotient at a nonzero level of the moment map. In each case, the $\Gamma$-Euler characteristic is expressed in terms of the weight matrix for the representation as well as a description of the abelianization of $\Gamma$.
