Hermitian K-theory for stable $\infty$-categories IV: Karoubi-Grothendieck-Witt theory

Baptiste Calm\`es, Emanuele Dotto, Yonatan Harpaz, Fabian Hebestreit, Markus Land, Kristian Moi, Denis Nardin, Thomas Nikolaus, Wolfgang Steimle · 2611.13248
author: Fabian Hebestreitauthor: Markus Landauthor: Denis Nardinauthor: Thomas Nikolaustitle: infinity-categorabstract: algebraic k-theormath.KTmath.ATmath.CT
Abstract

In analogy with the passage from connective to non-connective algebraic K-theory, Schlichting put forward the Karoubi-Grothendieck-Witt spectrum as an approximation to Grothendieck-Witt theory by a functor invariant under idempotent completion. In this paper, we extend his construction to the setting of Poincar\'e $\infty$-categories and characterise the resulting functor by the expected universal property. Our main result places these Karoubi-Grothendieck-Witt-spectra in an exact sequence involving the ultimately decorated L-spectrum and homotopy orbits of non-connective K-theory. This confirms a conjecture of Williams which had remained open even for rings in which $2$ is invertible, and allows us to deduce universal characterisations for ultimately decorated L-theory. We obtain these results by unifying the Bass-Heller-Swan and Ranicki-Shaneson splittings into a general decomposition theorem that yields, in particular, formulas for Grothendieck-Witt groups of Laurent polynomial rings. Finally, we construct multiplicative structures on both versions of our Grothendieck-Witt and L-functors and refine their universal properties to multiplicative ones. Along the way, we show that the original versions are even idempotent with respect to Day convolution.

Abstract

Let $n\geq0$ and let $\mathcal X$ be a bounded coherent $\infty$-topos. Suppose that finitely many noninitial coherent $n$-truncated objects cover its terminal object, and that every coherent $n$-truncated object in each of their slices has a finite covering family of sections. We identify $\mathcal X^{\mathrm{coh}}_{\leq n}$ with $\operatorname{Fun}(M,\mathcal S_{\pi,\leq n})$ for a $\pi$-finite $(n+1)$-type $M$, recovered by all finite models preserving finite limits, finite coproducts, and effective epimorphisms. Descent uses degrees through $n+2$ for objects and $n+1$ for maps; both bounds are sharp for finite homotopy actions. Every exact functor $F$ between recognized categories has a universal parameter $P_F=F_!(1)$ and a canonical factorization through its slice with fully faithful second functor. This parameter characterizes full faithfulness, conservativity, and all exact slice factorizations. For successive abelian Postnikov layers, we identify the space of marked exact lifts with a path space of Eilenberg--Mac Lane sections with local coefficients. The obstruction, classes of lifts, and relative automorphisms occur in degrees $r+1$, $r$, and $r-1$. We compute a twisted parity obstruction and, for odd primes, classify cyclic self-equivalence components by a $p$-adic valuation. A nonzero Postnikov class can persist in the identity component. For continuous finite families over a profinite $(n+1)$-type, we prove that a uniform finite-section atlas exists precisely when the complete model space is $\pi$-finite. Exact functors from finite model semantics, together with their lift spaces, satisfy finite-stage descent, while explicit profinite examples separate objectwise trivialization from uniform parameter elimination.

Vanishing Theorem of Pin(2)-equivariant Bauer-Furuta Invariants as Stable Sections

Haochen Qiu · 2611.13193
title: equivariantmath.GTmath.ATcross
Abstract

We develop a viewpoint of Pin(2)-equivariant Bauer-Furuta invariant: It can be converted to a section over a finite dimensional Pin(2)-orbit, and it takes values in ordinary stable homotopy class. This viewpoint reveals a topological limitation of the invariant: If the fiber's connectivity exceeds the dimension of the base space, then all sections are homotopic to the trivial section. We use it to recover Lin-Mukherjee vanishing theorem. Furthermore, the family Seiberg-Witten invariant can be obtained from the pullback of such sections to $S^1$-orbit. We use this fact to prove a vanishing theorem of the Pin(2)-equivariant family Bauer-Furuta invariant after two stabilizations.

Pre-Model Structures on the Category of $C_p$-Sets

David Mehrle, Ben Spitz · 2611.13407
abstract: goodwilliemath.CTmath.AT
Abstract

It is a folk theorem of Goodwillie that there are exactly 9 model structures on the category of sets, coming from 18 pre-model structures. We investigate model structures and pre-model structures on the category of $G$-sets for a finite group $G$. We outline a strategy for finding all model structures in this category, and partially execute this strategy for the case of $C_p$-sets, where $C_p$ denotes a cyclic group of prime order $p$. In particular, there are exactly 1777 pre-model structures on the category of $C_p$-sets.

The Ultimate Matching Theorem

Michael Goldman, Francesco Grotto, Dario Trevisan · 2611.00294
abstract: (infinity,2)math.PRHexagonAI-generated (no human author claimed)
Abstract

Let $X_1,\dots,X_N$ be independent uniform points in the unit square, matched either to an evenly spread deterministic configuration or to a second independent sample. For exponents $\alpha_1,\alpha_2>2$ with $1/\alpha_1+1/\alpha_2=1/2$, we prove that with probability tending to one a single permutation controls the $j$th coordinate displacements in the exponential Orlicz norm of exponent $\alpha_j$, at the scale $\sqrt{\log N/N}$, for both $j=1,2$. This settles Talagrand's Ultimate Matching Conjecture for all pairs of finite critical exponents. The proof constructs, on the flat torus, a current whose divergence is Gaussian white noise projected on a dyadic grid and whose coordinates have the critical anisotropic exponential Orlicz bounds, and transfers it to matching through a primal optimal-transport reduction. At the hard endpoints $(2,\infty)$ and $(\infty,2)$ the conjecture fails: a Brownian local-time construction shows that the normalized optimal matching cost diverges in probability.

Gaussian limits for non-cyclic and elementary abelian Sylow subgroups of the multiplicative group

Aimin Guo · 2611.13753
math.AT
Abstract

We study the number of non-cyclic Sylow subgroups of $(\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z)^\times$. Pollack suggested investigating whether this count has a limiting distribution after suitable normalization. For uniformly chosen $n\le x$, we prove a Gaussian limit with variance asymptotic to $\tfrac12\log_2x/\log_3x$. Motivated by the fixed-rank normal-order results of Morales, Polanco, and Pollack, we further prove joint Gaussian limits for this count and the numbers of elementary abelian Sylow subgroups of any fixed finite collection of ranks at least two. We give explicit prime-sum centers and asymptotics for all arithmetic covariances. The same joint theorem includes the number of common prime divisors of Euler's and Dedekind's functions. The proof uses the integer Kubilius model, estimates for the dependence between distinct output primes, and mean-square bounds for arithmetic truncation.

Geometric Hammocks: Surface Laminations and the Ziegler Spectrum of String Algebras

Shantanu Sardar · 2611.12931
math.RTmath.ATmath.DSmath.GT
Abstract

We give the first complete topological, geometric, and ergodic classification of the Ziegler spectrum $\Zg(\Lambda)$ of a non-domestic string algebra $\Lambda$ over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary cardinality, via a dictionary with Thurston's theory of geodesic laminations on a cusped hyperbolic surface. This is a model-theoretic counterpart of Haiden--Katzarkov--Kontsevich's realization (\emph{Publ.\ Math.\ IHES} 2017) of stability conditions on the bounded derived category of a gentle algebra via flat surfaces: pure-injective modules correspond to admissible geodesics, and the Ziegler topology to a corridor quotient of Chabauty space. Three consequences follow. The Krull--Gabriel dimension equals the surface's topological accumulation depth, and the string closure with all higher Cantor--Bendixson strata is globally homeomorphic to an explicit corridor space, with the pushout map to $\Zg(\Lambda)$ a homeomorphism if and only if $\Lambda$ is domestic. Prefix-free dyadic blocks in the geometric perfect core verify Puninski's cardinality-free two-chain criterion, resolving the open problem of superdecomposable pure-injectives over uncountable fields for all non-domestic string and special biserial algebras. Three purely algebraic invariants on the perfect core---localized pp-shape entropy, a $K_0$-weighted pressure function satisfying an algebraic Ruelle--Walters Variational Principle, and a pp-bifurcation index with a Gauss--Bonnet simplicity obstruction---strictly separate uncountable iso-entropic and iso-pressure families, and the inverse limit of the quotient pp-lattice recovers the transversal Cantor cross-section.

Linear circle quotients of products of odd-dimensional spheres: classification and geometric applications

Wen Shen, Yang Su · 2611.14144
math.DGmath.ATcross
Abstract

In this paper, we study the orbit manifolds of free diagonal linear circle actions on \(S^{2n+1}\times S^{2n+1}\). For every \(n\geq 1\), we establish a PL homeomorphism classification of the orbit manifolds in terms of the weights of the actions. We also obtain diffeomorphism classification and homotopy classification of such orbit manifolds in dimension $9$ and $13$. The diffeomorphism classification results lead to two geometric applications. For every \(m\geq 1\), there exist a non-spin \(9\)-manifold \(M_m^9\) and a spin \(13\)-manifold \(M_m^{13}\) in our family such that the moduli space of unit-volume homogeneous Einstein metrics on each manifold has at least \(2^{m-1}\) connected components. Moreover, each of these manifolds admits at least \(2^{m-1}\) regular Sasakian structures whose underlying contact structures are pairwise non-contactomorphic.

Thom Morphisms, Self-Intersection, and the Sectional Category of a Section

Paul-Eug\`ene Parent · 2611.14105
math.AT
Abstract

For sections between simply connected spaces of finite rational type, we compare module sectional category, sectional category and relative category after rationalization. Fibre Poincar\'e duality turns the compatibility of derived evaluation with the section into a derived self-intersection formula. We prove equality of the three invariants under any of four hypotheses: a base-dimension bound, finite total rational homotopy of the fibre, a smooth section with closed manifold fibre, or a compatible relative Frobenius model. Projective bundles over CP^k with fibre CP^r have common value k + r, which is unbounded even for fixed fibre. Whether fibre Poincar\'e duality alone suffices remains open.
