A higher categorical approach to graded bivariant $K$-theories for Real $C^*$-algebras

Anupam Datta · 2610.17060
title: higher categorabstract: infinity-categormath.KTmath.ATmath.OA
Abstract

We present an $\infty$-categorical refinement of classical $KK$-theory and $E$-theory for graded, $G$-equivariant for a locally compact second countable group $G$, Real $C^*$-algebras. As a consequence, we obtain a couple of fiber sequences connecting the different $K$-theory spectra, and explicitly analyse the induced endomorphisms on $K_0$-groups. We also answer precisely some folklore questions in the area. Among other things, we show that graded $KK$- and $E$-categories are not a Dwyer-Kan localization of graded $C^*$-algebras.

A non-nilpotent localization of a finite p-group

Sergei O. Ivanov, Georgii Kadantsev · 2610.16241
title: nilpotenmath.GRmath.CT
Abstract

We give negative answers to two questions of Farjoun about localizations of groups. For every odd prime $p$, we construct a finite $p$-group $G$, an infinite non-nilpotent group $H$, and a local embedding $f\colon G\hookrightarrow H$. The associated localization satisfies $L_f(G)\cong H$, showing that localizations need not preserve nilpotence and that the localization map $G\to L(G)$ need not be surjective when $G$ is a finite $p$-group.

Flat and Ghost dimensions in tensor triangulated categories with a compact tilting generator

Xiaoyan Yang · 2610.14420
title: tensor triangulatmath.CT
Abstract

Let $\mathcal{T}$ be a tensor triangulated category with a compact tilting generator $M$ and abelian heart $\mathcal{H}_M = M^{\bot_{\neq0}}$. We define a triangulated flat dimension $\mathrm{flatdim}_{\mathcal{T}}X$ for $X\in\mathcal{T}$, and give criteria in terms of tensor vanishing and resolutions by the class of pure quotients of $\mathrm{Add}(M)$. We further introduce $M$-ghost length and $M$-ghost dimension, and show that the global dimension of $\mathcal{H}_M$ controls the $M$-ghost length of all objects in $\mathcal{T}$, and that the $M$-ghost dimension of $\mathcal{T}$ coincides with the weak global dimension of $\mathcal{H}_M$ and the supremum of $\mathrm{flatdim}_{\mathcal{T}}X+\mathrm{inf}X$ of bounded objects, and the relative triangulated dimension of $\mathcal{T}^c$ with respect to $\mathrm{add}(M)$. This extends the classical Hovey-Lockridge theorem, which is recovered for derived categories of rings.

Local coherence and recollement of abelian categories

Mikhail Dusman, Alexander Pavlov · 2610.14931
abstract: presentablemath.CTmath.ACmath.RA
Abstract

We study how local coherenAAce of abelian categories behaves under gluing. For AB5 locally finitely presentable abelian categories, we give conditions on the gluing functors under which local coherence is transferred along a gluing $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}, \mathcal{C})$: from $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{C}$ to $\mathcal{B}$, and conversely from $\mathcal{B}$ to $\mathcal{A}$ or $\mathcal{C}$. The coherence theorem for augmented homotopes of Bondal and Zhdanovskiy is recovered as a special case, the coherence theorem for trivial extensions of Fossum, Griffith, and Reiten is a companion result in the same direction. We also introduce quasiprojective pairs of adjoint functors and prove a coherence criterion that generalises the criterion of Gomez Pardo and Martinez Hernandez to arbitrary Grothendieck categories. Finally, we extend Stenstr{ö}m's characterisation of coherence via FP-injective modules to compactly assembled Grothendieck categories: such a category is coherently assembled if and only if the class of CM-injective objects, defined through compact morphisms, is closed under filtered colimits. Along the way we revisit the various equivalent conditions for the coherence of a ring, collecting them in a single place. The proofs make use of the compactly assembled categories of Efimov.

p-adic Hodge theory of unipotent completions revisited

Ruth Wild · 2610.17169
abstract: syntomicmath.AGmath.NT
Abstract

Let $K/\mathbb{Q}_p$ be a finite extension, and let $X/K$ be an algebraic variety. We present a new proof that the unipotent completion of the étale fundamental group of $X_{\bar{K}}$ is a pro-de Rham representation, and show that it is pro-semi-stable whenever $X$ has semi-stable étale cohomology. We also reprove, using syntomic methods, that it is pro-crystalline at integral base points whenever $X$ admits a smooth proper formal model. All three proofs use the bar construction and are inspired by Wojtkowiak's approach to unipotent fundamental groups.

Characterization of $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor numbers of small rank

C-Y. Jean Chan, Thomas Hagedorn, Joel Louwsma, Michael Wijaya · 2610.14506
math.AGmath.AT
Abstract

The $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor number of a polynomial map is a quadratic enrichment of the classical Milnor number, defined as the local $\mathbb{A}^1$-Brouwer degree of the gradient. We study which elements of the Grothendieck-Witt ring of nondegenerate symmetric bilinear forms arise as $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor numbers. Over fields of characteristic not $2$, $3$, or $5$, we show that every element of the Grothendieck-Witt ring of rank at most $7$ that is a local $\mathbb{A}^1$-Brouwer degree is also an $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor number. Unlike for local $\mathbb{A}^1$-Brouwer degrees, however, in general not all $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor numbers can be obtained from two-variable polynomials. Over an arbitrary field of characteristic not $2$, $3$, $5$, or $7$, we characterize the elements of the Grothendieck-Witt ring of rank at most $8$ that are $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor numbers of two-variable polynomials. Over subfields of $\mathbb{R}$, this implies that there are local $\mathbb{A}^1$-Brouwer degrees of ranks $4$, $6$, and $8$ that are not the $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor number of a two-variable polynomial. We also prove that under a mild hypothesis one can reduce the number of variables of a polynomial while preserving the $\mathbb{A}^1$-Milnor number up to multiplication by a unit.

Geodesic Models for Loop Spaces on Riemannian Manifolds

Abbas Bahri, Fred Cohen, Moncef Ghazel, Sadok Kallel · 2610.15668
math.AT
Abstract

A topological group, defined in terms of ``composable minimal geodesics'' is constructed and shown to be homotopy equivalent to the pointed loop space of a path-connected, simply connected Riemannian manifold $M$ of positive convexity radius. This construction is analogous to John Milnor's free group model for the pointed loop space of a connected simplicial complex. Related models built from composable minimal geodesics are also obtained for the free loop space of $M$ and for the space of maps from any finite two-dimensional CW-complex into $M$.

Homotopy Self-Equivalences of $\mathbb{R}P^2\times S^2$ and a Braid for non-orientable $4$-Manifolds

Mehmetcik Pamuk · 2610.16606
math.GTmath.AT
Abstract

We compute the group of based homotopy classes of self-homotopy equivalences of the non-orientable $4$-manifold $\RP^2\times S^2$. It is an elementary abelian $2$-group of rank $5$. The subgroup of classes whose based lift to the universal cover $S^2\times S^2$ has degree $+1$ has rank $4$ and is isomorphic to the group of free homotopy classes. The group maps onto the group of self-equivalences of the algebraic $2$-type, which has order $8$, with kernel $π_4(\RP^2\times S^2)\cong(\Z/2)^2$ generated by pinch-fold maps; that these maps are independent is shown by an adjunction argument. We then construct a non-orientable analogue of the Hambleton--Kreck braid of exact sequences in dimension $4$, for non-orientable manifolds with spin universal cover, stated for finite fundamental group. We apply the braid to $\RP^2\times S^2$ and to $\RP^4$, and for the Hambleton--Hillman manifold with fundamental group $(\Z/2)^2$ we determine the normal $1$-type and show that self-equivalences act on $π_2$ through diagonal sign matrices. For $\RP^2\times S^2$ the pinch-fold maps are detected by twisted spin bordism, and, assuming facts from surgery theory stated as hypotheses, exactly one of the three non-trivial pinch-fold maps is homotopic to a homeomorphism.

Monophobic Neighborhoods of Cubes

Curtis Greene, Samira Sahar Jamil · 2610.14502
math.COmath.AT
Abstract

We define a $d$-cube in a graph $G$ to be monophobic in $G$ if, whenever it supports a $d$-face of a discrete singular $d+1$-cube, it must also support another face. In many cases, the presence of such cubes implies the nontriviality of discrete cubical homology and homotopy groups associated with $G$. We describe several large classes of examples and characterize the property completely when $d=2$.

Oka realizations, fundamental groups, and holomorphic cofibrations

Bin Guo, Yuta Kusakabe, Peng-Chao Wang · 2610.17044
math.CVmath.AGmath.AT
Abstract

The homotopy types of Oka manifolds are exactly those of countable finite-dimensional CW complexes. We construct realizations as open subsets of complex Euclidean spaces with explicit dimension bounds. Other realizations have good open covers by sets biholomorphic to $\C^N$, with prescribed finite nerves, or are unions of Fatou--Bieberbach domains whose nonempty finite intersections are again Fatou--Bieberbach domains. Every nonempty Stein manifold admits a proper holomorphic embedding into an Oka open subset of some $\C^N$, and this embedding is a homotopy equivalence. We obtain holomorphic representatives of ordinary and generalized cohomology classes, and realize every countable group as the fundamental group of an Oka domain in $\C^5$. The fundamental groups of algebraically elliptic manifolds are precisely the finite groups, and every virtually abelian Kähler group is the fundamental group of a projective Oka manifold. Finally, we classify holomorphic cofibrations in Lárusson's intermediate model structures and establish factorizations over aspherical targets into proper holomorphic embeddings and holomorphic fibre bundles with Oka fibre.

Representation homology of involutive algebras and hyperoctaherdral homology

Yuri Berest, Ajay C. Ramadoss, John Wood · 2610.14278
math.ATmath.KTmath.RT
Abstract

In this paper, we introduce and study the (involutive) representation homology of involutive algebras, following the development of representation homology of associative algebra in our earlier papers. Our main motivation is to understand the hyperoctahedral homology of involutive algebras. We show that over a field of characteristic $0$, the hyperoctahedral homology of an involutive algebra is isomorphic (as a graded commutative algebra) to its one dimensional involutive representation homology. This result enables the explicit computation of the hyperoctahedral homology of the universal enveloping algebras of involutive (DG) Lie algebras. For group algebras $k[Γ]$ equipped with the involution $g \mapsto g^{-1}$, we relate the involutive representation homology of $k[Γ]$ to the representation homology of the group $Γ$ with coefficients in $μ_2$, and we show that the latter is the homology of the loop space of the mod $2$ Dold-Thom space of $BΓ$; more generally, we compute the representation homology of $Γ$ with coefficients in an arbitrary diagonalizable group scheme. As a consequence, the hyperoctahedral homology of $k[Γ]$ is concentrated in degree $0$ in characteristic $0$, for every $Γ$.

Topological realizations of finite posets

Vin de Silva · 2610.15099
math.ATmath.GN
Abstract

I introduce a new class of cell complexes, called CL-complexes, that provide alternative topological realizations of finite posets. Each realization of a given poset has the same homotopy type as the order complex of that, so in some sense these spaces do not provide anything really new. Their value is that they enable short transparent proofs of standard results in poset homotopy. These include Quillen's Theorem A for finite posets, the Nerve Theorem for good covers of a topological space, and Sheehy's multicover generalization of the Nerve Theorem. Order complexes retain a special status: the order complex of a finite poset admits CL-complex structures both for the poset itself and for its opposite poset. The definition of CL-complexes is motivated by an elementary generalization of the Carrier Lemma of J. W. Walker, which in its original form constructs a canonical homotopy class of maps from a simplicial complex to a space covered by a suitable family of contractible sets. In the generalized lemma, the role played by simplicial complexes and the role played by spaces covered by contractible sets can both be fulfilled by CL-complexes. This sets up an equivalence of categories that tacitly underlies our approach.
