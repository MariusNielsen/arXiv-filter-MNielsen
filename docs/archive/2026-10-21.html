Complex Projective Spaces in Rational $SO(2)$-Equivariant Stable Homotopy Theory

Kian Lush · 2610.28039
title: stable homotopytitle: equivariantmath.AT
Abstract

We show that for almost every finite dimensional complex representation $W$ of $SO(2)$ the associated complex projective space $\mathbb{C}P(W)_+$ does not have the rational stable homotopy type of a specific wedge of representation spheres constructed from a chosen filtration of $\mathbb{C}P(W)_+$. This contrasts with the case for representations of finite Abelian groups where such an equivalence does hold. In the finite group case, the splitting result provides us with a classification result for projective spaces up to rational stable equivalence. In light of the non-splitting as a particular wedge of spheres in the case of projective spaces of $SO(2)$-representations, we also prove a separate classification theorem.

Prerna Dhankhar · 2610.27016
title: motivicabstract: motivic spectrabstract: algebraic k-theormath.ATmath.KT
Abstract

We compute the $2$-completed integral motivic homology, effective algebraic $K$-theory, and very effective Hermitian $K$-theory of the geometric classifying spaces $B\mu_{2^n}$, $n\geq 2$, over algebraically closed and real closed fields. We first carry out the corresponding classical calculations and, then obtain the motivic results using motivic spectral sequences and comparison techniques. The results reveal the classical $2$-torsion together with motivic $\tau$-periodicity and $\rho$-torsion.

Counterexamples to the Two-Bases Conjecture for the Tutte Polynomial

Jungang Chen · 2610.00434
abstract: presentablemath.COHexagonAI-generated (no human author claimed)
Abstract

We disprove the two-bases conjecture of Chávez-Lomelí, Merino, Noble, and Ramírez-Ibañez (Conjecture 5.8 in *Some Inequalities for the Tutte Polynomial*), which asserts that \(\max\{T_M(2,0),T_M(0,2)\}\ge T_M(1,1)\) whenever a matroid contains two disjoint bases or its ground set is the union of two bases. We construct a connected, self-dual, rationally representable matroid \(M\) of rank \(32\) on \(64\) elements whose ground set is partitioned into two bases, but for which \[ T_M(2,0)=T_M(0,2)=263656235940864 <275621102802945=T_M(1,1). \] The construction extends to an explicit infinite family \(M_m\) of rank \(2m\) on \(4m\) elements such that \[ \frac{\max\{T_{M_m}(2,0),T_{M_m}(0,2)\}}{T_{M_m}(1,1)}\longrightarrow0. \] Consequently, the corresponding additive and multiplicative Merino–Welsh inequalities also fail under the two-bases hypothesis. We additionally construct a simple and cosimple matroid on \(60\) elements whose ground set is partitioned into two bases and for which \(T_M(2,0)<T_M(1,1)\). This disproves the simple-matroid, two-bases-covering formulation of Conjecture 4.2 of Merino, Ibañez, and Rodríguez; its dual disproves the corresponding cosimple-matroid, two-disjoint-bases formulation. The constructions and inequalities are established by explicit rank functions, exact Tutte-polynomial evaluations, and elementary coefficient estimates.

Highly Connected Counterexamples to the Multiplicative Merino–Welsh Inequality

Jungang Chen · 2610.00430
abstract: presentablemath.COHexagonAI-generated (no human author claimed)
Abstract

For every integer \(k\ge 3\), we construct \(k\)-connected matroids representable over every infinite field, with girth \(k\), arbitrarily large cogirth, and Merino–Welsh quotient \[ \Phi(M)=\frac{T_M(1,1)^2}{T_M(2,0)T_M(0,2)} \] tending to infinity. In particular, this disproves **Conjecture 7.2 of Csikvári (2026)**, which asserts the multiplicative Merino–Welsh inequality for simple and cosimple matroids, and therefore also the stronger **Conjecture 7.3** therein, as observed by its author. Our examples are rank truncations of direct sums of \(k\)-element circuits. We give a 3-connected counterexample on 1011 elements of rank 433, certified by exact integer arithmetic, and a 6048-element counterexample admitting a proof without computation. Taking the direct sum of a counterexample with its dual also gives a negative answer to **Problem 4.3 of Beke, Csáji, Csikvári, and Pituk (2024)**: the original maximum and additive Merino–Welsh inequalities can fail for simple and cosimple matroids. The same construction disproves **Conjecture 2 of Knauer, Martínez-Sandoval, and Merino (2026)**: for every fixed \(g\ge 3\), \(\Phi\) is unbounded even among matroids having both girth and cogirth exactly \(g\). The dual direct-sum examples need not be connected; these constructions do not disprove the graphical or binary versions.

Quillen's local parameter conjecture in dimension four

Yunpeng Li · 2610.00404
abstract: motivic cohomologabstract: algebraic k-theormath.KTmath.ACHexagonAI-generated (no human author claimed)
Abstract

Let $R$ be a Noetherian regular local ring of mixed characteristic and dimension four, and let $r$ be a member of a regular system of parameters. We prove that every finitely generated projective module over $R[1/r]$ is free. We also prove that $\mathrm{Sp}_4(R[1/r])$ acts transitively on unimodular columns of length four and that every determinant orientation of a rank-two projective module is induced by a basis. The argument reduces freeness to two vanishing results: the degree-three Zariski cohomology of the naive degree-four Milnor--Witt sheaf and the alternating Witt group of $R[1/r]$. We establish these results using integral degree-two algebraic $K$-theory, supported hermitian $K$-theory, and local motivic cohomology. A finite-rank symplectic comparison relates the image of the completion obstruction to Milnor--Witt multiplication and yields the required symplectic frames.

The stacky approach to de Rham-Witt cohomology

Yuanning Zhang · 2610.28149
abstract: topological restriction homologyabstract: genuine r-abstract: infinity-categormath.AGmath.ATmath.NT
Abstract

Let $k$ be a perfect field of characteristic $p>0$. Ekedahl proved an equivalence of triangulated categories between bounded coherent complexes over the Raynaud ring and complexes of $F$-gauges. We give a geometric proof, independent of his proof, that the de Rham-Witt realization is a symmetric monoidal equivalence of stable $\infty$-categories between perfect $F$-gauges and bounded coherent complexes over the Raynaud ring with Ekedahl's completed star product, as expected by Illusie. The geometry comes from topological restriction homology: $\mathrm{TR}^r(k;\mathbf Z_p)^{hS^1}$ is an iterated fibre product of copies of $\mathrm{TC}^-(k;\mathbf Z_p)$, and taking even stacks before and after the fibre product gives the genuine and the naive $r$-Nygaard stacks. Pushforward from the genuine to the naive $r$-Nygaard stack realizes Andriopoulos' $r$-Nygaard filtrations, which interpolate between the Nygaard filtration at $r=1$ and the Hodge-Witt filtration at $r=\infty$. On the genuine $r$-Nygaard stack, which is a chain of Nygaard charts, restriction and Frobenius come from the inclusions of sub-chains, Verschiebung from duality, and the differential as a Bockstein operator, and the relations of the star product become projection formulas. As applications, we deduce Ekedahl's K\"unneth formula and Poincar\'e duality for de Rham-Witt cohomology from their counterparts for $F$-gauge cohomology, and show that the special-value formulas of Illusie and of Mondal over finite fields are equivalent.

In this note, we elaborate on a result of Krannich--Kupers--Mehzer on Disc-presheaves. Dold--Kan Correspondences via Morita Normalization

Florian Warg · 2610.28138
math.CTmath.ATmath.KTmath.QA
Abstract

The classical Dold--Kan correspondence identifies simplicial modules with connective chain complexes. Using Morita theory, we construct a Dold--Kan-type correspondence for every linear category carrying a suitable action of the maximum-preserving simplicial operators. The construction functorially assigns a Morita-equivalent normalized category to every such linear category. In particular, we obtain Dold--Kan-type correspondences for all crossed simplicial groups. We prove a rank formula for the normalized hom-modules and show that a crossed simplicial group is self-dual precisely when its underlying simplicial set of automorphism groups has dimension one. Furthermore, we construct normalization-compatible self-dualities and recover the Dwyer--Kan correspondence for duplicial, paracyclic and N-cyclic modules.

Probabilistic Stability Transfer for One-Dimensional Euler Characteristic Transforms

Vanja Stojanovi\'c · 2610.26870
math.ATmath.PR
Abstract

The Euler characteristic transform (ECT) is a computable topological signature for shapes embedded in Euclidean space. Existing ECT stability results control changes in the transform through deterministic geometric perturbation bounds, but many data-generating mechanisms produce perturbations that are random and unbounded in the worst case. In such settings a uniform deterministic bound may be unavailable or too pessimistic. This paper formulates a probabilistic stability-transfer principle for one-dimensional ECT stability: a deterministic stability estimate is combined with a tail bound for the induced geometric error to obtain a high-probability ECT error bound. We instantiate this principle using the stability theorem of Marsh and Beers for embeddings of finite one-dimensional CW complexes. The resulting consequences cover ambient displacement fields conditionally on admissibility and supremum-tail estimates, and finite landmark perturbations with reconstruction. For landmark perturbations, bounded, sub-Gaussian, sub-exponential, and polynomial-tail noise assumptions give explicit high-probability ECT stability bounds. The framework isolates a geometric stochastic component that arises in shape analysis pipelines across biological morphology, medical imaging, molecular structure analysis, and remote sensing.
