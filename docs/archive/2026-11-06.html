Exotic smooth structures on complex projective spaces

Yueshi Hou, Hana Jia Kong, Zhouli Xu · 2611.08406
author: Zhouli Xuabstract: chromatic homotopymath.AT
Abstract

We study smooth structures on the topological manifold underlying complex projective space. We conjecture that every complex projective space $\mathbb{CP}^n$ with $n>3$ admits an exotic smooth structure. We prove two results providing strong evidence for this conjecture. First, the set of complex dimensions $n$ for which $\mathbb{CP}^n$ admits an exotic smooth structure has natural density one. Second, we explicitly identify $266$ congruence classes modulo $288$ such that $\mathbb{CP}^n$ admits an exotic smooth structure whenever $n>3$ lies in one of these classes. These classes cover more than $92\%$ of complex dimensions, improving the previously known lower bound of approximately $36\%$. We prove these results by first relating the concordance group of $\mathbb{CP}^n$ to suitable Atiyah--Hirzebruch filtration subgroups of its stable cohomotopy group, and then applying computations in chromatic homotopy theory at height $2$.

Higher Noncommutative Motives and Higher K-theory

Hisato Matsukawa · 2611.07262
abstract: (infinity,n)math.KTmath.ATmath.CT
Abstract

We construct higher additive noncommutative motives of stable $(\infty,n)$-categories, extending the additive motives of Blumberg, Gepner, and Tabuada and the secondary theory of Mazel-Gee and Stern. We formulate a notion of exact sequences and establish the universal property of the resulting additive motives. We give two equivalent constructions: a localization of enriched presheaves and a weighted Waldhausen $S$-construction on complete exact flags. We then compute their Hom objects, obtaining ordinary K-theory at the first level and lower additive motives recursively at higher levels. Our results recover the classical and secondary theories, provide coherent multiplicative structures, and yield an endofunctor on the category of Stefanich rings. In short, higher motives are higher K-theory.

Braided Multisections and Akhmedov's Fake Quadrics

Anar Akhmedov · 2611.06949
math.GTmath.ATcross
Abstract

We give handlebody decompositions of the complements of the cyclic multisections in $\Sigma_g\times S^2$ constructed in~\cite{AB}. The 2-handle framings are induced by the product coordinates. We compute the integral homology of the complements from their handle chain complexes, including the torsion subgroup of $H_2$. For the degree pairs $(3,3)$ and $(2,4)$, we specify surface diffeomorphisms inducing the maps on first homology prescribed in~\cite{AY} and recover the integral first homology groups $(\Z/3)^2\oplus\Z/(3m)$ and $(\Z/2)^2\oplus\Z/(4m)$, respectively, for $m\geq1$. For the symmetric $m=1$ sum, we also give a simultaneous surface diagram of the relative product attachments and identify their product framings with the surface framings. The resulting symplectic sums are aspherical and symplectically minimal, and have the rational cohomology ring of $S^2\times S^2$.

Multipartite Entanglement, TQFT Partition Functions and PL Bordism

Michele Del Zotto, Abhijit Gadde, Pavel Putrov · 2611.07000
hep-thcond-mat.str-elmath-phmath.ATcross
Abstract

In a previous work we conjectured that the multi-invariants of the ground state of a gapped $(d-1)+1$-dimensional system, labeled by bipartite $(d+1)$-edge-colored graphs encoding closed oriented $d$-manifolds $M$, compute the partition functions $Z(M)$ of the low energy topological quantum field theory (TQFT), up to non-universal factors determined by the local structure of the triangulation. In this paper we determine which combinations of partition functions can be isolated from the non-universal factors. The answer is governed by oriented bordism. If $M$ is null-bordant, $Z(M)$ is obtained from an explicit, state-independent finite family of multi-invariants, up to a power of $Z(S^d)$ which is fixed to be $Z(S^d)^{\chi(M)/2}$ for even $d$ and can be removed for odd $d$. Conversely, the obstruction is measured by a quotient of the group of "residues" of colored graphs which receives a surjective map from the oriented bordism group, with kernel precisely the torsion subgroup as far as smooth manifolds are concerned. In $d=4$ the obstruction is the signature: $Z(M)/Z(S^4)^{\chi(M)/2}$ can be extracted if and only if $\sigma(M)=0$. This is due to the fact that the first Pontryagin class $p_1$ has a local expression and thus $e^{i\theta\sigma(M)}=e^{\frac{i}{3}\theta\int_M p_1}$ cannot be separated from the non-universal factors. Torsion invariants, in contrast, are not obstructed: for the ground state of the $4+1$-dimensional invertible phase with partition function $(-1)^{\int w_2w_3}$ we compute the multi-invariants exactly, and two graphs with identical local data, encoding the Dold manifold and $\mathbb{CP}^2\times S^1$, give opposite signs.

On bordism classes of symmetric products of surfaces

Georgy Chernykh, Dmitry Gugnin · 2611.07813
math.AT
Abstract

We explicitly determine the complex bordism class of the $n$-th symmetric product of a complex curve of genus $g$. In particular, we provide an explicit formula expressing this class as an integral linear combination of bordism classes of products of complex projective spaces. Similarly, we determine the unoriented bordism class of the $n$-th symmetric product of a non-orientable surface of genus $g$ (i.e., $\#_{i=1}^{g+1}\mathbb R P^2$). It turns out that these classes are also expressed as a $\mathbb Z_2$-linear combination of bordism classes of products of even-dimensional real projective spaces. We also give an affirmative answer to a non-orientable version of the Blagojevi\'c--Gruji\'c--\v{Z}ivaljevi\'c conjecture stating that the topological type of any symmetric product of a noncompact surface of finite type determines the topological type of the surface itself.

On the Topology of the Set of Nonnegative Forms That Are Not Sums of Squares

Maximilian Illmer, Tim Netzer · 2611.07252
math.ATmath.AG
Abstract

We study the topology of the normalized space of real nonnegative forms of degree $2d$ in $n$ variables that are not sums of squares. Whenever it is nonempty, we prove that it is path-connected but not acyclic, and in particular not contractible. We give explicit bounds on the degrees in which its reduced homology can be nonzero. In the two minimal nonempty cases, we show an embedding of an exterior algebra into the cohomology ring, and prove containment of the binary octahedral and binary icosahedral groups, respectively, in its fundamental group.

Shades and Convexity of Hypersurfaces

Mohammad Ghomi · 2611.08415
math.DGmath.ATcross
Abstract

Let $f\colon M^n\to\mathbf{R}^{n+1}$, $n\geq2$, be a smooth immersion of a closed oriented manifold, with Gauss map $\nu\colon M\to\mathbf{S}^n$. For each unit vector $u$, the shade $S_u\subset M$ is the set of points where $\nu$ makes an acute angle with $u$. We show that $f$ is a convex embedding if and only if every shade is contractible. More generally, if every shade is $k$-connected, then $f$ is a convex embedding provided that $n\leq 2k+1$, or $n=2k+2$ and $H_{k+1}(M;\mathbf{Z})=0$. On the other hand, for every $n\geq 2k+3$ there is a nonconvex embedding $f\colon\mathbf{S}^n\to\mathbf{R}^{n+1}$ all of whose shades are $k$-connected. These results give sharp answers to a question of H. Wente in all dimensions.

The universal property of the decomposition space of quasisymmetric functions

Philip Hackney, Joachim Kock, Jan Steinebrunner · 2611.08063
math.CTmath.ATmath.CO
Abstract

Aguiar, Bergeron, and Sottile showed that the coalgebra of quasisymmetric functions is terminal among connected graded coalgebras with a zeta function. After developing the notions of gradings and zeta functions for decomposition spaces, we establish an objective homotopic version of this result. Namely, the decomposition space $Q$ of quasisymmetric functions is terminal: for any conservatively graded decomposition space $X$ with zeta function $F$, there is a unique graded ikeo-culf span $X \leftarrow W \rightarrow Q$ inducing $F$ from the special zeta function on $Q$. More generally, for gradings in any complete decomposition space $M$ with indecomposable identities, the terminal object is $J(M)$, the free decomposition space on the nondegenerate simplices of $M$; the quasisymmetric case is $Q=J(B\mathbb{N})$.

ToroFit: Fitting Wedge Sums of Tori to High-Dimensional Data

Tanishq Bhatia, Jose A. Perea, Tarek M. Zikry · 2611.06990
stat.MLcs.LGmath.ATcross
Abstract

Data arising in scientific applications often exhibits complex nonlinear structure. Identifying and parameterizing said nonlinearities can enable better modeling, enhance predictive analysis, data interpolation, denoising, and anomaly detection. In this paper, we develop theoretical and computational methods to detect and parametrize Euclidean point cloud data lying near a wedge sum of tori. Specifically, we use persistent cohomology and cup products to identify the distinct toroidal components, and on each component, we optimize over low-energy multivariate trigonometric polynomials for best data fit. The ensuing optimization problem reduces to generalized ridge regression, which can be solved efficiently via standard techniques. We demonstrate the utility of the proposed framework in both synthetic and real datasets, including interpolation of low frame-rate recurrent videos, attractor recovery in quasiperiodic dynamical systems, and the parametrization of gene-space cycle data for parasites and humans.
