The spectrum of units of algebraic $K$-theory

Shachar Carmeli, Kiran Luecke · 2410.10126
author: Shachar Carmelititle: algebraic k-theormath.KTmath.ATreplace
Abstract

It is well known that the $[0,1]$ and $[0,2]$ Postnikov truncations of the units of the topological $K$-theories $\glone \KO$ and $\glone \KU$, respectively, are split, and that the splitting is provided by the ($\Z/2$-graded) line bundles. In this paper we give a similar splitting for the $[0,1]$-truncation of the units of algebraic $K$-theory, considered as a sheaf on affine schemes. A crucial step is to produce the splitting for $\glone K(\Z)$. Along the way we also give a complete calculation of the connective spectrum of strict units of $K(\Z)$ and $K(\F_\ell)$ for a prime $\ell$. Finally, we show that the units of algebraic $K$-theory do not split as a presheaf. In fact we show they do not even split pointwise.

Anti-successors and anti-replicators, Manin products and Koszul duality

Chengming Bai, Li Guo, Yunhe Sheng, You Wang · 2610.21365
title: koszul dualmath.RAmath-phmath.ATmath.MPcross
Abstract

We develop an operadic theory of anti-splitting and anti-replication for binary operads. For a binary operad $\opd$, we define its anti-bisuccessor $\ABSu(\opd)$ and anti-trisuccessor $\ATSu(\opd)$, with the property that an operation of $\opd$ splits into a negative sum of two or three operations, yielding canonical operad morphisms $\opd \to\ABSu(\opd)$ and $\opd\to\ATSu(\opd)$. For binary quadratic operads, the process gives Manin black products, \[ \ABSu(\opd) \cong \antiprelie \bullet \opd, \qquad \ATSu(\opd)\cong \antipostlie\bullet\opd. \] Dually, we introduce the anti-duplicator \(\ADup(\mathcal P)\) and anti-triplicator \(\ATrip(\mathcal P)\) and prove \[ \ADup(\opd^!)\cong \ABSu(\opd)^!, \qquad \ATrip(\opd^!)\cong \ATSu(\opd)^!. \] Consequently, the anti-duplicator gives a new way to calculate Manin white products. %, in particular, $\ADup(\opd)\cong \antiperm\circ\opd$. The framework recovers known anti-splittings, including anti-dendriform, anti-pre-Lie, and anti-pre-Poisson structures, in a uniform operadic form and produces further examples and dual pairs.

Equivariant $K$-theory as a global localization

Stefan Schwede · 2610.21675
title: equivariantabstract: ring spectrabstract: infinity-categormath.AT
Abstract

We establish a global equivariant refinement of the theorem of Snaith that presents the complex $K$-theory spectrum as a localization of the suspension spectrum of $\mathbb C P^\infty$. We prove that the morphism of ultra-commutative global ring spectra from $\Sigma^\infty_+ B_{\text{gl}} U(1)$ that classifies the tautological $U(1)$-representation exhibits the global $K$-theory spectrum as a specific localization, in both the $\infty$-categories of ultra-commutative ring spectra and of commutative global ring spectra. The classes to be inverted are certain representation-graded $U(n)$-equivariant homotopy classes of $\Sigma^\infty_+ B_{\text{gl}} U(1)$, the pre-Bott classes. We also show that for every compact Lie group $G$, the $G$-equivariant $K$-theory spectrum is a telescopic localization of $\Sigma^\infty_+ B_G U(1)$ by the pre-Bott classes of all unitary $G$-representations.

Local Tate duality for syntomic cohomology in positive characteristic

Christopher Lazda · 2610.21622
title: syntomicmath.NTmath.AGcross
Abstract

Let $k$ be a local field of characteristic $p>0$. We show that syntomic cohomology over $k$, with coefficients, and defined using Dodd's theory of $F$-gauges, satisfies local Tate duality. We also prove that syntomic cohomology over the ring of integers of $k$, again with coefficients, is Lagrangian with respect to the duality pairing. Finally, we show that, for $p>2$, these results recover Shatz's duality and Milne's orthogonality results for the cohomology of finite flat group schemes of $p$-power order over $k$.

Nilpotent Deformation Theory via Ultrasolid Algebra

Sof\'ia Marlasca Aparicio · 2610.20801
title: nilpotenmath.AGmath.AT
Abstract

Deformation theory studies how varieties and other algebro-geometric objects vary in families. A central part of the subject is formal deformation theory, where one deforms over an Artinian base; such deformation problems are governed by Lie algebraic models. We pose the question of deforming varieties over nilpotent but not necessarily Artinian bases. These turn out to be classified by the same Lie algebraic models plus some topological structure. More precisely, we will consider partition Lie algebroids in the category of ultrasolid modules, a variation of the solid modules of Clausen and Scholze that give a well-behaved category akin to topological modules. To approach this result, we decompose deformation problems into n-nilpotent layers. Each of these layers is individually easier to understand, and is classified by simpler variants of partition Lie algebras.

Normed idempotence of spectra and distributivity in equivariant higher algebra

Kaif Hilman, Branko Juran, Natalie Stewart · 2610.22074
title: higher algebratitle: equivariantabstract: genuine g-spectrabstract: g-spectrabstract: presentablemath.AT
Abstract

We introduce and develop the theory of distributivity for base categories equipped with so-called atomic orbital subcategories, with an eye towards applications in equivariant homotopy theory. This extends the important framework in Nardin's thesis and supplies several crucial details on distributivity missing from the treatment therein. As our main technical result, we provide a parametrised distributive version of the universal property of "partial cocompletions with relations". We then employ this to construct a distributively normed symmetric monoidal structure on presentable categories. In turn, by specialising to the case of equivariant homotopy theory for compact Lie groups G, we show that the category of genuine G-spectra is normed idempotent, so that we obtain the structure of multiplicative norms on G-spectra along inclusions of finite index subgroups.

The invariant cell number property for $\mathbb{E}_4$-rings

Anish Chedalavada, Florian Riedel · 2610.22162
title: e_4-ringabstract: infinity-categormath.AT
Abstract

For any non-zero $\mathbb{E}_4$-ring $R$, we prove that an $n$-fold direct sum of suspensions of $R$ cannot admit an exhaustive filtration by fewer than $n$ cells. We do this by showing that certain integral representations of the symmetric group lift to Schur functors on additively $\mathbb{E}_3$-monoidal $\infty$-categories. We then reduce the statement to a vanishing fact for rational Schur functors due to Deligne.

Unbounded Nilpotency Gaps on Rationally Elliptic Manifolds

Paul-Eug\`ene Parent · 2610.21280
title: nilpotenmath.AT
Abstract

We construct simply connected, closed smooth manifolds whose rational homotopy types are elliptic and whose homology nilpotency exceeds rational category by arbitrarily large amounts. This separates short models from short quotients of minimal models. The construction starts from an explicit family of minimal Sullivan algebras with finite-dimensional cohomology. A finite quotient establishes Poincar\'e duality and gives an upper bound for homology nilpotency, while a closed orientation functional computes rational category. Two forced primitives yield the lower bound. An even regrading separates the relevant weights, extending this obstruction to every graded acyclic differential ideal. Rational surgery realizes the resulting models in their formal dimensions, with vanishing rational Pontryagin classes and arbitrarily large rational connectivity. The ratio of homology nilpotency to rational category tends to four thirds. Finally, under an external spherical stabilization statement, attaching a single cone can increase homology nilpotency by an arbitrarily large amount while leaving rational category unchanged.

The real Betti realization of very effective Hermitian K-theory

Julie Bannwart · 2610.20688
abstract: ring spectrabstract: e_1-ringabstract: motivic spectrabstract: algebraic k-theormath.AT
Abstract

Real Betti realization is a symmetric monoidal functor from the category of motivic spectra to that of topological spectra, extending the functor that associates to a scheme over $\mathbb{R}$ the space of its real points. In this article, we identify the $\mathcal{E}_1$-ring structure on the real realization of $\mathsf{ko}$, the very effective cover of Hermitian K-theory $\mathsf{KO}$, by an explicit 2-local fracture square, as being equivalent to $\mathsf{L}(\mathbb{R})_{\geq 0}$, the connective L-theory spectrum of $\mathbb{R}$. Along the way, we also compute the real realization of other motivic ring spectra: motivic cohomology $\mathsf{H}\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathsf{H}\mathbb{Z}/2$, the variant $\widetilde{\mathsf{H}\mathbb{Z}}$, $\mathsf{KO}$, the very effective cover $\mathsf{kgl}$ of algebraic K-theory $\mathsf{KGL}$.

A counterexample to a conjecture of Plotnick on knot exteriors

Tommy Hofmann, John Nicholson · 2610.21051
math.GTmath.ATmath.NTcross
Abstract

We resolve a conjecture of S. Plotnick from 1983 concerning the homotopy type of four-dimensional knot complements. We show that the exteriors of two branched twist spins of the trefoil, which were shown by Plotnick not to be homotopy equivalent as boundary pairs and hence not homeomorphic, are in fact simple homotopy equivalent. The main ingredient of the proof is the construction of an explicit element of augmentation $49$ in the integral group ring of $\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb{F}_5)$ which is a unit modulo the group norm and commutes with the element inducing the monodromy on the fundamental group.

Global transfer systems and endopurity of subgroups

Scott Balchin, Miguel Barrero · 2610.22141
math.ATmath.GR
Abstract

Global transfer systems are combinatorial objects that parametrize intermediate levels of structures and properties in globally equivariant homotopy theory. In this paper we study the lattice of global transfer systems, expanding beyond the case of abelian groups which was computed by the second author. The main result is a proof that the irreducible elements of the lattice of global transfer systems are strictly controlled by subgroups of groups which we refer to as endopure. From the irreducible elements one can recover the whole lattice, and studying these endopure subgroups leads us to a wide range of new phenomena and computations.

Integral homology lattices of matching and circulant complexes

Qihang Wang, Weiye Li · 2610.22082
math.COmath.ATcross
Abstract

Bouc's hook cycles span the rational top homology of the matching complex of $K_{2k+1}$. We prove that their integral saturation quotient has exponent exactly $2^{\lfloor k/2\rfloor}$ for every $k\geq1$, and construct divided-power cycles attaining this bound. For $k\geq2$, the homology lattice is not isomorphic to the standard integral hook lattice as an $\mathfrak S_{2k+1}$-module. For circulant complexes, we determine the integral dihedral homology modules of $\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3s}^{s-1})$, $\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3s+1}^{s-1})$ and $\operatorname{Ind}(C_{3s+2}^{s-1})$, for $s\geq3$, $s\geq2$ and $s\geq3$, respectively, and classify the least scalar lifts of all primitive equivariant quotients to the rank-one module with trivial rotation and negative reflection. Tate localization relates prime-order gluing to fixed-point homology under a free, single-degree homology hypothesis. Explicit cycle subgroups separate saturation defects from obstructions to equivariant sections. For circulant independence complexes with jumps two and three, we prove an eight-periodic ordinary homotopy classification and a fixed-space obstruction to uniform representation suspensions. For every fixed nonempty bounded positive jump set, a finite-state construction gives a rational critical-face series.

Postnikov invariants for rational spaces

Clovis Chabertier · 2610.21953
math.AT
Abstract

In this paper we construct a theory of Postnikov invariants for (complete) homotopy Lie algebras that is preserved by the integration functor of Robert-Nicoud and Vallette. This result is in the same vein as Berglund's theorem, which relates the homology groups underlying a nilpotent homotopy Lie algebra to the homotopy groups of the associated space. Resolutions of orbifold generalised triangle groups

Sean P. Carroll, Thomas Csizmadia, Jeroen Schillewaert · 2610.20932
math.GRmath.ATmath.GTcross
Abstract

Let $T=\langle f,g\mid f^{l}=g^{m}=W^{n}=1\rangle$ be a generalised triangle group in which $W$ is the Farey word of a rational slope $q=r/s$, and suppose that $T$ is the fundamental group of a hyperbolic $3$-orbifold whose singular locus has no spherical vertices. Let $G=\langle f,g\mid f^{l}=g^{m}=W^{n}\rangle$ be the associated central extension. We compute an explicit free resolution of the trivial module over $\mathbb{Z}G$ of minimal length, with differentials given in closed form by Fox derivatives. From this resolution we deduce an explicit free resolution over $\mathbb{Z}T$ which is $2$-periodic in degrees at least two, and we compute the integral homology and cohomology of both groups. Strebel treated the classical family $q=0/1$ in 1983. Since then only the family $q=1/2$ has been treated, by Ellis and Williams, and for that family our resolution over $\mathbb{Z}T$ has strictly smaller rank in every degree.
