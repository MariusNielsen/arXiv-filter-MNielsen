Interest profile (backtest version): Marius Nielsen (FU Berlin), stable and chromatic homotopy theory

This version deliberately contains only what was known before he reviewed the backtest
listings, so the test measures whether the recommender finds papers on its own.

Core
- Synthetic spectra and their variants (Syn_E, filtered/synthetic deformations, Pstrągowski's framework); descendability, nilpotence exponents, nilpotent completeness; derived categories of E(n)_*E(n)-comodules, Franke-style algebraicity.
- Adams and Adams–Novikov spectral sequences, descent spectral sequences, chromatic homotopy (Morava E/K-theory, K(n)/E(n)-local categories, Lubin–Tate theory, telescope conjecture, redshift).
- Filtered spectra and filtered algebraic geometry: Postnikov/Whitehead filtrations, flat topologies on (filtered) ring spectra, spectral and filtered stacks, quasi-coherent sheaves, Dirac geometry, even filtration, BMS motivic filtration.
- Trace methods: THH, TC, cyclotomic/cyclonic/polygonic spectra, prismatic cohomology.
- Tensor-triangular geometry: Balmer spectra, smashing and telescope questions. Own paper on the formal spectrum of a tt-category (with Heard).
- Exact and stable infinity-categories: stable envelopes, K-theory of exact categories (own paper with Winges); hopfological algebra (own paper with Gómez and Jasso).
- Sheaves valued in spaces on topological spaces and the functoriality of X ↦ Shv(X).
- Higher category theory as a working tool: presentable and stable infinity-categories.

Not interested
- Topological data analysis and persistence.
- Pure algebraic geometry without a homotopical angle (moduli of curves, birational geometry, Picard numbers, enumerative geometry).
- Representation theory of quivers, Lie algebras, automorphic forms, unless there is a tt-geometric, stable-categorical or homotopical angle.
