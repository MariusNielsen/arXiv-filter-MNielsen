Interest profile: Marius Nielsen (FU Berlin), stable and chromatic homotopy theory

Core (recommend readily)
- Synthetic spectra and their variants (Syn_E, filtered/synthetic deformations, Pstrągowski's framework); descendability, nilpotence exponents, nilpotent completeness; derived categories of E(n)_*E(n)-comodules, Franke-style algebraicity.
- Adams and Adams–Novikov spectral sequences, descent spectral sequences, chromatic homotopy (Morava E/K-theory, K(n)/T(n)/E(n)-local categories, Lubin–Tate theory, telescope conjecture, chromatic splitting, redshift).
- Filtered spectra and filtered algebraic geometry: Postnikov/Whitehead filtrations, flat topologies on (filtered) ring spectra, spectral and filtered stacks, quasi-coherent sheaves, Dirac geometry, even filtration, BMS motivic filtration.
- Trace methods: THH, TC, cyclotomic/cyclonic/polygonic spectra, prismatic and syntomic cohomology.
- Tensor-triangular geometry: Balmer spectra, smashing and telescope questions, stratification. Own paper on the formal spectrum of a tt-category (with Heard).
- Exact and stable infinity-categories: stable envelopes, K-theory of exact categories (own paper with Winges); hopfological algebra (own paper with Gómez and Jasso).
- Higher category theory as a working tool: presentable and stable infinity-categories, (op)lax limits, straightening, factorization systems, parametrized/equivariant/global infinity-categories, sheaves of spaces and functoriality of Shv(X).

Also wanted
- Classical stable homotopy theory: stable stems, Hopf/Kervaire invariant, Adams conjecture, image of J, ring spectra and obstruction theory (Goerss–Hopkins).
- Equivariant and global homotopy theory (Mackey functors, genuine G-spectra, equivariant chromatic computations).
- Condensed and pyknotic mathematics applied to homotopy theory; six-functor formalisms on the homotopical side.
- Everything posted or cross-listed to math.AT is shown anyway; recommend from it only when it fits the core.

Not interested
- Topological data analysis and persistence (shown via math.AT, never recommend).
- Pure algebraic geometry without a homotopical angle (moduli of curves, birational geometry, Picard numbers, enumerative geometry).
- Representation theory of quivers, Lie algebras, automorphic forms, unless there is a tt-geometric, stable-categorical or homotopical angle.
